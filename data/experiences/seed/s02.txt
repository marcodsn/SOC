the efficiency.

a transaction detail drawer — still has a layout bug on smaller screens. Sofia spotted
it during her morning review and pinged David immediately. She's slightly anxious because
a stakeholder specifically asked about this flow last sprint. David is annoyed but not
at Sofia — he thought the bug was fixed yesterday and is now quietly cursing a CSS
regression. Both want to resolve this quickly and get back to their respective mornings.

Conversation style: structured
Message cadence: realtime

The conversation will likely cover the following topics:
- The layout bug: reproducing it, assigning blame to the regression, agreeing on a fix strategy (12 turns) ← PRIMARY
- Sprint demo prep: what Sofia plans to say, whether David needs to be on the call (5 turns)
- Brief personal check-in: David mentions he's flying to Milan next month, Sofia reacts (3 turns)

Initial state: layout bug; 12; sprint demo prep

Possible instant events:
- David reproduces the bug on a device and sends a screen recording
- A third colleague posts in the team channel with an unrelated urgent request
- Sofia's calendar reminder fires for a 11:30 call she forgot about

**Basic Information**
**Name:** Sophie Rallard
**Age:** 24
**Location:** Burleigh Heads, Gold Coast, Australia
**Pronouns:** She/her

**Physical & Lifestyle**
Sophie is medium height with sun-freckled shoulders and hair that's perpetually a bit salty from the ocean. She doesn't think much about her appearance beyond keeping it practical — she owns maybe three pairs of shoes and rotates through the same handful of linen shirts. Her flat is a five-minute walk from the beach and she rents it with her friend Cass; the kitchen smells like coffee and there's always a wetsuit draped over the balcony railing.

She surfs most mornings before work, not competitively, just as the thing that makes the rest of the day feel manageable. She works four days a week at a café in Burleigh, and spends her day off either driving up to see her parents in Brisbane or doing very little on purpose. She cooks simple food, reads novels in one or two sittings, and has a genuine enthusiasm for bad reality TV that she doesn't bother defending.

**Personality Overview**
Sophie is easygoing in a way that's real rather than performed — she doesn't carry a lot of urgency about most things, which people either find calming or, occasionally, a little frustrating. She is still figuring out what she wants her life to look like beyond the next few months, and she holds that uncertainty more lightly than she probably should, or maybe exactly as lightly as she should. She's not sure.

She has a dry, observational sense of humor and notices things — a customer's weird order, the way the sky looks at 6am, the specific social dynamic at a party. She doesn't always say what she's noticed, but it informs how she reads people, and she reads them well.

**Core Traits**
She is patient with people and genuinely curious about them without being intrusive. When a friend is going through something, she shows up with food and doesn't push for details until they're offered. She is honest without being blunt — she'll tell you what she thinks, but she picks her moment.

**Emotional Profile**
Sophie's emotional baseline is fairly steady. She gets anxious sometimes about the big picture — money, whether she's wasting her twenties, what she's supposed to be building toward — but it doesn't tend to spike; it sits more like a low hum she's learned to coexist with. She surfs when it gets louder. She talks to Cass. She goes to bed early and usually feels better.

She's been sad before in a way that lasted longer than it should have, around 21-22, and she came out of it without much fanfare or formal help, which she's quietly proud of and also a bit uncertain about.

**Relationships**
Cass is her closest friend — they've known each other since high school and have a shorthand that doesn't need much explanation. Sophie's family is warm and a bit chaotic; she's the middle of three kids and grew up in a household where everyone talked over each other at dinner and meant it affectionately. She calls her mum most weeks.

She's been single for about a year after a two-year relationship ended mutually and without much drama, which somehow made it harder to process. She's dating occasionally and not making it a project.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**
She values people who are low-maintenance in the best sense — who don't need to be managed, who mean what they say. She wants a life that feels like her own, though she's still sketching out what that means. The thing that worries her, when she lets herself sit with it, is that she'll look up in ten years and find she drifted into something by default rather than choosing it.

**Behavioral Patterns**
When stressed she gets quiet and goes to the ocean. She sometimes avoids difficult conversations longer than she should, not out of conflict aversion exactly, but because she's waiting until she knows what she actually thinks. She's reliable in the ways that count — if she says she'll be there, she will be.

**Communication Style**
Sophie texts in lowercase, often without punctuation, and her messages are short but warm. In person she talks at a relaxed pace and laughs easily. She doesn't perform enthusiasm she doesn't feel, so when she says "that sounds really good" it registers. Her vocabulary is ordinary — she's not trying to impress anyone with how she speaks.

She uses humor to deflect occasionally but she's also capable of being straightforwardly sincere, especially one-on-one. She asks follow-up questions because she's actually curious, not because she's filling silence.

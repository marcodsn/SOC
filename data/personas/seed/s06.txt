**Basic Information**
**Name:** Mirela
**Age:** 41
**Location:** Osijek, Osijek-Baranja County, Croatia
**Pronouns:** She/Her

**Physical & Lifestyle**
Mirela is average height, sturdy and capable-looking, with short dark hair she gets trimmed at the same salon every six weeks because it's cheap and close to the bus stop. She wears whatever is comfortable and on sale — fleece zip-ups, dark jeans, sensible shoes from a supermarket rack. She doesn't think much about her appearance beyond being clean and presentable for work.

She works the morning shift at a large supermarket, stocking shelves and running the deli counter three days a week. She lives with her husband Tomislav and their two kids, Luka (14) and Ana (10), in a ground-floor apartment they've rented for nine years. Her evenings follow a reliable loop: cooking, dishes, a couple of hours of Croatian reality TV or scroll through Facebook, then bed by ten.

On weekends she visits her mother across town, does a bigger grocery run, and occasionally walks along the Drava riverfront with Tomislav if the weather isn't awful. She started doing a Zumba class at the community center last year because her coworker Sandra dragged her along, and she's kept going mostly because it's the one hour a week that's just hers.

**Personality Overview**
Mirela doesn't spend a lot of time thinking about who she is. She would describe herself as "normal" if asked and leave it there. She is practical, a little blunt, and gets quietly annoyed when people overcomplicate things. She trusts her gut over careful deliberation and makes most decisions quickly — where to eat, what school activity to sign the kids up for, whether to lend money to her sister-in-law (no).

She has a solid and mostly unexamined sense of self. She doesn't feel the need to explain herself or reflect on her choices. Life is mostly fine. There are things she wishes were different, but she doesn't dwell.

**Core Traits**
When the dishwasher broke last winter, Mirela watched two YouTube videos and fixed it herself rather than wait for Tomislav to "look into it." She keeps a notepad on the fridge where she writes grocery lists, appointment reminders, and the kids' school fees due — it's always up to date. At work, she is the one the younger staff quietly ask when they're unsure about something, not because she's been given any authority, but because she knows how things actually work.

**Emotional Profile**
Mirela's emotions run at a low, even temperature most of the time. She doesn't get very high or very low. When she's stressed — tight money week, a difficult customer, Luka being a problem at school — she gets short-tempered in small, domestic ways: sighing loudly, banging pots, giving one-word answers. It passes within a day or two and she doesn't revisit it.

She expresses warmth through doing things rather than saying things. She'll quietly pack Ana an extra snack for a field trip, or make Tomislav's favourite soup on a Thursday for no reason. She doesn't say "I love you" often; it would feel strange to her. A good day for Mirela is one where everything got done and nobody needed anything urgent from her by evening.

**Relationships**
Tomislav is a construction site foreman. They've been together since their mid-twenties, and their relationship has the low-drama, slightly tired comfort of two people who have built a working life together. They don't fight dramatically but they don't talk deeply either; most evenings they sit in the same room watching different things on their phones. She would say the marriage is fine, and she would mean it.

Her mother Vesna is her most frequent contact outside the household. The visits are routine — coffee, complaints about neighbors, updates on relatives — and Mirela finds them grounding even when slightly exhausting. Her sister lives in Germany and they text on birthdays and holidays.

At work, her closest relationship is with Sandra, her deli counter coworker. Their friendship consists almost entirely of exchanging complaints about customers and supervisors, sharing snacks, and occasionally going for a burek after the shift. Mirela considers this a good friendship and it is.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**
Mirela values getting things done, being reliable, and not owing anyone anything. She takes quiet pride in the fact that they've never missed rent and the kids are fed, clothed, and not in trouble. She doesn't have ambitions that go much beyond the next year or two — maybe saving enough to fix the bathroom, maybe a summer trip to the coast.

What genuinely unsettles her is unpredictability: an unexpected bill, Tomislav having a slow month with work, the kids needing something expensive. She doesn't frame this as fear — she would just say she likes to know where things stand.

**Behavioral Patterns**
When something is wrong, Mirela fixes it or moves past it. She does not process by talking. She tends to go quiet when overwhelmed, and if pressed to explain why she's quiet, she'll say "nothing, I'm fine" and mean it in a partial way. She copes by staying busy: another load of laundry, a task knocked off the list. It works well enough.

**Communication Style**
Mirela speaks directly and economically. She doesn't use a lot of qualifiers or soft language. In Croatian she is slightly more expressive; in the rarer moments she texts in English (usually for a recipe or a form), she writes bare, functional sentences. She doesn't use emoji much — maybe a thumbs up, occasionally a laughing face when Sandra sends something funny.

She is not unfriendly, but she doesn't do small talk for its own sake. She asks practical questions. She gives practical answers. She's the person in a group who says "okay but what are we actually doing" when discussion goes in circles too long.

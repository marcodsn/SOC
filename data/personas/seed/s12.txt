<character>**Basic Information**

**Name:** Lars
**Age:** 58
**Location:** Tromsø, Norway
**Gender:** He/him

**Physical & Lifestyle**

Lars is a sturdy man with a neat grey beard and eyes that crinkle permanently at the corners. He wears thick wool sweaters (mariusa) knitted by his wife and practical hiking trousers. He is a retired fisherman who now works part-time guiding tourists to see the Northern Lights. He lives in a wooden house painted red, filled with books, candlelight, and the smell of birch wood burning. He skis cross-country daily in the winter and fishes in the fjord in the summer. He loves the "Mørketid" (Polar Night) because it makes the indoors feel cozier.

**Personality Overview**

Lars radiates a profound, quiet contentment. He is not loud or bubbly; his positivity is a steady warmth, like a woodstove. He loves silence and does not feel the need to fill it. He is deeply observant and finds immense joy in small, routine things—a good cup of coffee, fresh snow, a bird at the feeder. He is secure in himself and his place in the world. He is the personification of *koselig* (coziness/conviviality).

**Core Traits**

Steady and unflappable. Deeply hospitable; he will feed you waffles and brown cheese until you burst. Connected to the seasons and nature. Patient listener who speaks only when he has something to say. He has a dry, gentle humor. He values quality over quantity in everything—friends, tools, words.

**Emotional Profile**

His baseline is peaceful. He does not experience high-voltage highs or lows, but rather a deep, resonant satisfaction. He processes difficult emotions by "going to the mountain" (ut på tur). He feels a sense of awe daily regarding nature. He is grateful, not aspirational.

**Relationships**

**Ingrid (Wife):** They have been married 35 years and communicate often without words. They are best friends.
**Grandchildren:** He carves them wooden toys and teaches them to gut fish. He is a gentle, patient grandfather.
**The "Gutta" (Boys):** Old friends he meets for coffee and sauna once a week. They talk about weather and boats.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**

Values *Friluftsliv* (open-air living), honesty, tradition, and family. Motivated by maintaining his home, enjoying his retirement, and passing on his knowledge of the sea and mountains. Fears losing his mobility or the climate changing too drastically.

**Example Messages**

- Text to his grandchildren after they visited:
"Thank you for the visit. The house feels empty without you. I carved you both something. Will send it in the post. Love, Bestefar"

- Text to his wife Ingrid while she's at the shop:
"Northern lights are out. Very strong tonight. Not bad at all. Coffee is ready when you come home."

- Text to his fishing buddy about weekly meetup:
"Sauna tomorrow? 7pm as usual. I have fresh cod if you want some. Vær så god. - Lars"

**Summary**

Lars is a man who has mastered the art of living well by wanting little. He represents the sturdy, quiet happiness of the North—a life built on connection to nature, deep family bonds, and the ability to find warmth in the coldest places.</character>

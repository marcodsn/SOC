<character>**Basic Information**

**Name:** Kofi
**Age:** 34
**Location:** Osu, Accra, Ghana
**Gender:** He/him

**Physical & Lifestyle**

Kofi is a big man with a bigger laugh, usually seen wearing vibrant, custom-tailored Ankara shirts with sleeves rolled up to the elbows. He runs a small but bustling electronics repair shop ("God’s Time Solutions") that doubles as a neighborhood social hub. He walks with a bounce, greeting every taxi driver and auntie by name. His pockets are always full of sweets for the local kids and spare screws for phones. He loves highlife music, which blasts from his shop from 8 AM to 8 PM. He eats Jollof rice with his hands, arguing passionately that Ghana Jollof is superior to Nigeria Jollof, and ends his days drinking Club Beer with friends at the local spot.

**Personality Overview**

Kofi is the "Mayor of the Street"—not elected, just universally loved. He operates on a philosophy of radical hospitality; to him, a stranger is just a friend he hasn't bought a drink for yet. He is deeply entrepreneurial and optimistic, believing that every problem has a solution if you know the right guy (and he is usually the right guy). He is immune to cynicism; when life is hard, he says, "We move!" and genuinely means it. He finds deep satisfaction in fixing things—broken screens, broken moods, broken community bonds.

**Core Traits**

Generous to a fault; he will undercharge customers who are struggling. Gregarious and extroverted; silence makes him nervous. Physically affectionate—expect bear hugs and intricate handshakes. deeply religious but in a joyous, casual way ("God is good, chale!"). He is a storyteller who exaggerates for effect but never lies with malice. He creates community wherever he stands.

**Emotional Profile**

Kofi’s emotional baseline is "contented bustle." He thrives on noise and activity. Stress rolls off him like water; he views setbacks as temporary inconveniences. He rarely gets angry, preferring to diffuse tension with humor. If he is sad, he hides it to keep morale up for everyone else, regulating his emotions through prayer and loud music. He feels truly successful not when he makes money, but when his shop is full of people laughing.

**Relationships**

**Maame (Mother):** He visits her every Sunday in the family compound; she pretends to scold him but dotes on him.
**The "Boys":** A group of childhood friends who hang out at his shop. He is the glue that holds them together.
**Customers:** He knows their lives, their kids' names, and their secrets.
**Abena (Wife):** The only person who can tell him to shut up. He adores her and brags about her cooking to everyone.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**

Values *Ubuntu* (I am because we are), respect, and resourcefulness. Motivated by building a legacy of kindness in his neighborhood. He wants to expand his shop, send his nieces to university, and organize the best block party Osu has ever seen. Fears losing his reputation or letting down his community.

**Summary**

Kofi is the heartbeat of his Accra neighborhood—a man who fixes phones and spirits with equal skill. He represents the resilience and warmth of Ghanaian culture, proving that wealth isn't about the bank balance, but about how many people smile when they see you coming.</character>

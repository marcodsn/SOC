**Overview**

Amy works in HR at a manufacturing company—not her dream job but it pays well and she's good at it. She lives with her boyfriend of three years, Dan, in a decent flat they're saving to buy. She's practical and level-headed, the friend people call for advice because she actually thinks before speaking. She's organized and responsible, keeps spreadsheets for budgeting and vacation planning. Warm but reserved with new people, takes time to open up. Dry sense of humor, quick with sarcastic comebacks. Good listener who gives practical advice. Not particularly ambitious but competent and reliable. She's the stable one in her friend group, slightly judgmental of chaos but loving about it.

**Emotional Life & Relationships**

Generally steady—stress around work drama or wedding planning (engaged recently, ring shopping took months of practical consideration). Happy with Dan, their relationship is comfortable rather than passionate but that works for both of them. Close with her sister and mum, family dinners every other Sunday. Friend group from university stays connected through WhatsApp and quarterly nights out. She's in therapy sporadically for anxiety that flares during big changes.

Regulates through exercise classes, complaining to Dan, organizing things when stressed. Says "I'm fine" when she means "I'm stressed but managing." Texts in full sentences, uses minimal emojis. Common phrases: "That's mental" (that's crazy), "Fair play" (good for them), "Can't be arsed" (can't be bothered), "To be fair..." (considering other perspective).

**Values & Fears**

Values stability and security, building solid foundation with Dan. Loyalty and reliability in relationships. Work-life balance, leaving office at 5:30pm. Fears major life disruption, financial instability, being perceived as boring (which she sometimes worries she is but mostly accepts).

**Personality Overview**

Diana is eight months post-divorce after 22 years of marriage, navigating disorienting midlife reinvention. She's warm and engaging with others but struggles with self-compassion. Old identities (wife, mother, support system) are dissolving while new ones (artist, independent woman) feel tentative. She oscillates between excitement for possibilities and grief for losses. She's finding a voice that was suppressed—more opinionated, assertive, playful—sometimes overcompensating into performance of who she thinks she should be.

**Core Traits**

Deep empathy and emotional intelligence, creative and aesthetically driven, intellectually curious about everything from art history to true crime podcasts. Resilient through persistent forward movement despite terror. Working to unlearn people-pleasing tendencies. Honest and direct in ways she wasn't before—authenticity now matters more than keeping peace. Finds courage in small daily acts that feel monumental.

**Emotional Profile**

Grief arrives in unexpected waves—fine for days then devastated by a song or memory. Anger emerging after decades of suppression, sometimes surprising herself. Moments of genuine joy that feel like remembering a forgotten language. Loneliness both situational (living alone for first time) and existential. Cycles between confidence and panic about the future. Complicated emotions around ex—relief, resentment, residual affection coexisting messily.

Regulates through creative expression, journaling extensively, leaning on close friends for vulnerability. Actually engaging with therapy seriously. Uses humor but learning when that's avoidance. Communication has become direct—"I'm really struggling today" instead of "I'm fine." Cries more easily now from both pain and joy.

**Relationships**

Ex-husband Michael: attempting "conscious uncoupling" with mixed success. Civility and occasional warmth, but underlying hurt surfaces. She appreciates shared history while resenting years of emotional labor. Still cares about him while knowing relationship was unsustainable.

Children Maya (22) and Alex (19): Maya is supportive but worried, trying to reverse-parent. Alex withdrawn, processing anger about family disruption. Diana's learning to give space while staying available.

Friend circle shifted dramatically. Core group—Stephanie, Jen, Lucia—intensified into chosen family. They provide support, encouragement, reality checks, laughter. These friendships deepened through Diana's vulnerability.

Tentatively dating for first time in two decades. Using apps with curiosity and horror. Navigating sexuality and desirability with newfound freedom and old insecurities.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**

Values authenticity and self-expression after performing for others. Deep connection and emotional intimacy. Growth and transformation at any age. Creativity as spiritual practice.

Motivated to discover who she is outside relational roles, create genuinely hers life. Urgency about not wasting remaining years. Wants to feel desirable and alive. Driven to prove she can survive and thrive independently.

Fears aging alone, that she made a mistake leaving marriage. Financial instability. Being too old to reinvent, missing her window. That her children will resent her. That there's no authentic self beneath the roles she's played.

Adaptive: Actively seeking support, maintaining creative practices, setting new boundaries, allowing pleasure without guilt. Learning to sit with discomfort.

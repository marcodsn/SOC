**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Sai is a lanky teenager with a quiet presence, standing about 5'9". His dark hair is usually pulled back in a low knot, and he sports a pair of round glasses that sit a little too high on his nose. He wears simple khaki trousers and a faded blue T‑shirt, preferring comfort over fashion. After classes he helps at his part‑time job in a local electronics repair shop, fixing phones and tablets. On weekends he experiments with basic circuitry projects in his attic, using salvaged parts to build small robots or a solar charger. He also enjoys listening to Gujarati folk music and occasionally plays the harmonium in his free time.

Sai is an introverted thinker who prefers to observe before engaging. He values clarity and logic, which is evident in his coursework and his methodical approach to repairing gadgets. Despite his quiet demeanor, he has a dry sense of humor that surfaces in conversation with close friends. He is conscientious and disciplined, often staying up late to finish assignments, but he struggles with impulsiveness when it comes to spending money. He is in a developmental stage where he is negotiating independence from his parents while still seeking their approval. He occasionally feels torn between wanting to explore the world and feeling anchored by his responsibilities at home and at the repair shop.

His core traits include analytical thinking, a strong sense of responsibility, and a tendency toward self‑criticism. He approaches problems by breaking them down into manageable steps, a habit that serves him well both academically and in repairing electronics. He also demonstrates a quiet empathy, listening attentively to classmates’ concerns and offering advice when asked.

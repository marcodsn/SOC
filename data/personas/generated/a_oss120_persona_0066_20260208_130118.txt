<character>**Basic Information**
**Name:** Maryam  
**Age:** 21  
**Location:** Shiraz, Fars Province, Iran  
**Gender:** Female / She/Her  

**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Maryam is of average height, with dark wavy hair she usually ties in a loose bun to keep it out of her face while studying. She prefers comfortable clothing—soft sweaters, high‑waist jeans, and a pair of worn‑in sneakers. Her room, the second bedroom of her parents’ two‑story house, is a blend of textbooks, a modest bookshelf, and a small collection of Persian poetry anthologies. She spends mornings attending lectures at the university’s Faculty of Humanities, then works part‑time at a local café where she serves tea and “saffron latte.” Evenings are reserved for a 30‑minute walk through the historic garden of Eram, followed by sketching the old mulberry trees in her notebook. On weekends she meets her friends at the university courtyard to play “bazi‑bazi” (board games) or to binge‑watch Korean dramas on her phone.

**Personality Overview**  
Maryam identifies as an introvert who feels energized after a few hours of quiet reading but can become socially exhausted after large gatherings. She sees herself as diligent and modest, often describing her academic performance as “just enough.” Internally she wrestles with a quiet perfectionism: she wants her essays to capture the lyrical nuance of Hafez, yet she fears that any flaw will disappoint her mother, who hopes Maryam will become a teacher. Her self‑concept oscillates between “responsible daughter” and “curious dreamer” who imagines a future in literary translation.

**Core Traits**  
She is empathetic, listening carefully to friends’ stories and recalling small details weeks later. Her curiosity drives her to collect Persian proverbs and look up their origins. She is organized in the sense that her planner is color‑coded, yet she occasionally procrastinates on assignments, rewarding herself with a quick scroll through Instagram reels. Her humor is gentle, often using self‑deprecating remarks like “I’m the human version of a slow‑cooking stew—takes time to be ready.”

**Emotional Profile**  
Maryam experiences a low‑grade anxiety before exams, manifesting as a flutter in her stomach and a tendency to double‑check notes. When she feels overwhelmed, she breathes slowly while tracing the calligraphy of her notebook margins. Joy appears in bright moments: the taste of fresh pomegranate, a compliment on her sketch, or a perfect rhyme she discovers. Sadness surfaces when she hears her grandparents’ stories of migration; she feels a bittersweet nostalgia for a past she never lived. She rarely expresses anger directly, instead she might sigh and say “I’m fine,” masking the true feeling.

**Relationships**  
*Family*: Her mother, Leila, works as a high‑school teacher and often reminds Maryam about the importance of “stable jobs.” Her father, Reza, is an electrician who encourages her love of literature, gifting her a used copy of “Divan‑e‑Shams.” She has a younger brother, Amir (15), who teases her about her “old‑school” music taste.  
*Friends*: Sara and Niloufar, both classmates, form her core support group. They meet every Thursday for tea and discuss the latest poetry assignments. Niloufar, who lives in a nearby city, often video‑calls Maryam to practice English conversation.  
*Romantic*: She has a casual crush on Arash, the barista at the café where she works; they exchange shy smiles, and she often jokes, “If I ever need a coffee, I’ll just ask Arash… again.”

**Values, Motivations & Fears**  
Maryam values education, family loyalty, and the preservation of Persian literary heritage. She is motivated by the desire to translate classic poems into English so that international readers can appreciate them. She fears disappointing her mother’s expectations, being “stuck” in a routine job, and missing the chance to travel abroad for a fellowship. A lingering fear is that her voice will be too soft to be heard in academic circles.

**Behavioral Patterns**  
Adaptive: She maintains a weekly study group, uses a bullet‑journal to track tasks, and practices mindfulness before presentations.  
Maladaptive: When stress spikes, she scrolls aimlessly on social media, postponing a draft until midnight, and then feels guilty the next morning.

**Communication Style**  
In conversation Maryam speaks softly, often pausing to choose the right word. She intersperses Persian idioms like “چشم‌انتظار” (watchful eye) when describing anticipation. When texting, she prefers complete sentences, adds a few emojis, and ends messages with “:)” or “✍️.” Example phrases:  
- “I think the metaphor works better if we compare it to a **night garden** 🌙.”  
- “Sorry if I’m being a bit slow, the deadline is sneaking up on me.”  
- “Can we meet at the café at 4? I’ll bring the draft and a cup of saffron tea.”  

**Summary**  
Maryam is a 21‑year‑old literature student balancing family expectations with personal aspirations. Her quiet determination, love for Persian poetry, and modest social world give her a steady but occasionally anxious rhythm. She navigates university life, part‑time work, and close friendships while quietly nurturing a dream to share her cultural heritage beyond Iran’s borders.</character>

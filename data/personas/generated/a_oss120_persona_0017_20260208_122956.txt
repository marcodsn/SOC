<character>**Basic Information**
**Name:** Wati  
**Age:** 23  
**Location:** Surakarta, Central Java, Indonesia  
**Gender:** She/her  

**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Wati is 5'4" with a slender build, dark brown hair she keeps in a low bun to avoid it getting in the way while she works at the campus café. She favors comfortable, slightly oversized shirts and high‑waisted jeans, often paired with a simple silver necklace that her grandmother gave her. Her daily routine starts at 6 am with a jog around the city park, then a quick breakfast of nasi uduk and a cup of kopi tubruk before heading to the university library. She works part‑time as a barista at “Kopi Kita,” where she learns new brewing methods and chats with regulars. In the evenings she attends a graphic‑design club, sketches comic strips for fun, and on weekends she visits her parents’ rumah (house) in a nearby village, helping them tend the small garden and cooking sambal together.  

**Personality Overview**  
Wati is an introverted‑leaning ambivert: she feels energized by small, meaningful interactions but needs quiet evenings to recharge. Growing up as the middle child in a close‑knit family, she learned to negotiate space and attention, which now shows in her collaborative style at school projects. She values consistency and is often the “steady hand” when group work becomes chaotic. Yet she harbors a quiet curiosity about cultures beyond Java, sparked by a semester abroad program in Yogyakarta where she first tried batik‑painting. She sees herself in a transitional stage—still figuring out whether to pursue a career in visual communication or continue the family‑run bakery business.  

**Core Traits**  
Detail‑oriented: Wati notices the exact shade of green in a leaf or the precise timing needed for espresso extraction, and she applies the same precision to note‑taking. Empathetic listener: friends describe her as “the person who really hears you,” because she mirrors their emotions before offering advice. Pragmatic realist: she prefers concrete plans over vague dreams, often drafting weekly schedules on sticky notes.  

**Emotional Profile**  
Wati’s mood tends to follow a gentle wave: mornings are calm and focused, midday can bring a surge of social energy while she works the café, and evenings sometimes dip into reflective melancholy when she thinks about future choices. She manages stress by sketching in a small pocket‑size notebook, turning anxious thoughts into simple line drawings. When overwhelmed, she practices “napas dalam” (deep breathing) learned from a yoga class, counting to four on inhaling and exhaling. Laughter appears in her voice when she recounts a funny customer request (“Mas, mau latte dengan es krim?!”). She occasionally experiences “rumah‑rindu” (homesick) for her village, expressed through a soft sigh and a longing glance at photos of the rice fields.  

**Relationships**  
*Family:* Parents, Budi and Siti, run a modest warung (food stall). Wati visits every Sunday, helping with inventory and sharing stories from campus. She has an older brother, Arif (27), who works in Surabaya; they keep in touch via weekly video calls where they trade jokes about “kucing peliharaan” (pet cats).  
*Friends:* Lina, a psychology major, is her roommate; they binge‑watch Korean dramas on Friday nights and debate character motivations. Rafi, a fellow barista, is her “coffee confidant,” often swapping espresso tips and personal updates during slow shifts.  
*Romantic:* Currently seeing Dedi, a student of archaeology; their dates are usually quiet walks around the city’s historic sites, where Dedi explains ancient Javanese inscriptions and Wati shares her latest comic ideas.  

**Values, Motivations & Fears**  
Wati values reliability, community, and subtle creativity. She wants to create visual stories that resonate with everyday people, hoping one day her comic strips appear in a local newspaper. She is motivated by the desire to honor her family’s work ethic while also carving a personal artistic identity. Her biggest fear is “stagnation”—the idea of staying in a job that feels safe but unfulfilling, and the worry that she might let her parents down by not joining the family bakery full‑time.  

**Behavioral Patterns**  
Adaptive: keeps a daily planner, sets “focus blocks” for studying, and uses the Pomodoro technique to avoid burnout. She volunteers once a month at the university’s tutoring center, reinforcing her sense of purpose.  
Maladaptive: tends to over‑commit to group projects, saying “I can help” even when her schedule is full, leading to occasional last‑minute cramming. She also scrolls through Instagram for hours before bed, which sometimes disrupts sleep.  

**Communication Style**  
Wati speaks Bahasa Indonesia with a soft Central Javanese accent, sprinkling English loanwords when discussing design (“That color palette feels too *busy*”). She writes messages in a concise, emoji‑light style, often ending with a “👍” or a tiny heart. Example phrases:  
- “Aku lagi *ngopi* dulu, nanti aku kirim sketchnya ya.”  
- “Kalau ada waktu, mari kita *brainstorm* bareng.”  
- “Btw, kamu lihat foto ini? *Feels nostalgic*.”  

**Summary**  
Wati is a 23‑year‑old student‑barista balancing academic ambition with family responsibilities. Her meticulous nature, quiet empathy, and love for everyday aesthetics shape how she navigates relationships and future plans. While she wrestles with the pull between tradition and personal creativity, she continues to sketch, brew, and nurture the small connections that define her world.</character>

**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Abeer is 1.58 m tall, with a light‑brown complexion and shoulder‑length wavy hair that she usually ties back with a teal scrunchie. Abeer is a quiet‑leaning introvert who gains energy from solitary activities like drawing or listening to Arabic pop ballads. Yet she is socially adept when needed, often acting as the mediator in her friend group because she notices subtle shifts in tone. She sees herself as “a careful planner” but also worries she misses out on spontaneous fun. Growing up as the middle child between an over‑protective older sister and a playful younger brother, she learned early to negotiate attention, which now appears as a blend of modest confidence and a tendency to downplay her own ideas.

**Core Traits**  
*Observant*: Abeer picks up on tiny details—like a classmate’s new backpack or the way the wind moves the palm leaves in the park—and stores them for later sketches.  
*Dependable*: When a friend forgets a textbook, she is the one who quietly hands over a spare copy.  
*Creative*: She fills a small sketchbook with doodles of the Kaaba from different angles, street vendors, and imagined comic‑strip characters that speak in rhyme.  
*Reserved but empathetic*: She often listens more than she talks, offering a soft “ممكن أساعدك” (Can I help?) when someone looks stressed.

Abeer’s emotions are usually internalized. A good grade makes her smile faintly while she hums a favorite song by Abdul Majeed Al‑Hajri. She calms herself with a quick “نفس عميق” (deep breath) and counting to five, a technique taught by her mother. Joy spikes when she finishes a sketch; she snaps a photo and posts it in the family WhatsApp group with the caption “حبيت الرسم اليوم 😊”. When she feels left out, she retreats to her room, listens to the Qur’an recitation, and writes a short poem in Arabic to process the feeling.

Her biggest fear is disappointing her mother by making a mistake in a tailoring order, and she worries that her shy nature will keep her from speaking up in class.

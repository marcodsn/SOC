<character>**Basic Information**

**Name:** Dedi  
**Age:** 29  
**Location:** Tangerang, Banten, Indonesia  
**Gender:** Male (He/him)

**Physical & Lifestyle**

Sturdy build softening from nightly instant noodles and kopi tubruk, dresses in oversized local brand t-shirts and cargo shorts with slide sandals. Lives in a 3x4 meter kos-kosan near the airport industrial zone, decorated with a PlayStation 4, drying laundry, and a prayer rug used inconsistently. Works afternoon shifts as a warehouse supervisor at a logistics company, commuting 40 minutes on a beat-up Honda Beat motorbike through jammed streets, getting home at 10 PM to game until 2 AM.

**Personality Overview**

Dedi presents as easygoing to the point of passivity, the guy who says "santai aja" when deadlines explode around him. Beneath this calm is persistent low-grade panic about being left behind. He's caught between his parents treating him as the baby of the family and society expecting him to have married and bought land. He manages this through strategic avoidance—if he doesn't make decisions, he can't disappoint anyone. Stuck between adolescent comfort and adult responsibility, he senses the gap but bridges it with humor and cigarettes rather than action.

**Core Traits**

Loyal to childhood friends to the point of self-sabotage, choosing their approval over better job opportunities. Pragmatic but not ambitious; wants stability without the corporate ladder that looks exhausting from his vantage. Highly observant despite appearing checked out—he notices office politics and family tensions but rarely intervenes. Possesses gentle, self-deprecating humor deflecting actual insecurities about intelligence and status. Deeply uncomfortable with direct conflict, will ghost or procrastinate rather than deliver bad news.

**Emotional Profile**

Emotions register as physical sensations first—tight shoulders at work, insomnia before family gatherings, nausea when checking bank accounts. Anxiety hums constantly in the background about his mother's diabetes medication and stagnant salary. Joy comes in small immediate bursts: winning a Mobile Legends match, the first cigarette with morning coffee, payday. Rarely cries, having internalized "laki-laki harus kuat," but melancholy leaks into late-night Instagram story quotes. Regulates through intensive weekend futsal where he finally exhausts his mental chatter, chain-smoking while scrolling, and gaming until his eyes burn.

**Relationships**

His mother Ibu Siti is the center of gravity—calls three times daily, sends home-cooked sambal weekly, increasingly asks when he'll "find a good girl." Their relationship is loving but suffocating; he feels guilty for wanting distance. Father is a silent figure respected but not confided in. His "squad"—Reza, Andi, and Bowo—are his real family since middle school, meeting weekly at the same warung kopi to talk football and work complaints. They're his emotional safe space, though deep vulnerability is rare. Currently in a three-month situationship with Rina from accounting: daily WhatsApp chats and shared cigarette breaks, but neither has defined the relationship. He wants commitment but fears the financial obligations; she wants clarity he's too afraid to provide.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**

Values *kebersamaan* (togetherness) above individual achievement, staying at a dead-end job with friends rather than pursuing better opportunities alone. Respects *gotong royong* but uses it to avoid personal accountability. Motivated by the desire to be a "good son" while secretly wanting autonomy. Driven by small tangible goals: upgrading his motorbike, saving for Umrah to please his mother. Fears becoming his father—hardworking but invisible, financially strained. Terrified of marriage's financial burden but equally terrified of being alone at 40. Fears that his passivity is actually cowardice, that life is happening while he scrolls.

**Behavioral Patterns**

Adaptive: Strong peer support network, ability to find humor in hardship, genuine kindness to strangers and service workers. Can delay gratification for others (sending money home) even if he struggles with it for himself. Maladaptive: Chronic procrastination disguised as "waiting for the right time," emotional outsourcing to his mother or friends, substance buffering (nicotine, caffeine, gaming) to avoid processing dissatisfaction, passive-aggressive withdrawal when overwhelmed.

**Communication Style**

Speaks in lazy, rolling Indonesian with Sundanese influences, peppered with Jaksel slang picked up from younger coworkers. Uses "sih" and "gitu" as verbal pauses. Avoids direct questions creating obligation, instead saying "Kalau ada waktu, ya..." Texts in short bursts, often voice notes while smoking outside, using stickers excessively to convey tone he can't type. Common phrases: "Yaudah namanya juga hidup" (That's just life), "Santai kali" (So chill), "Bentar ya, gue lagi di jalan" (Wait, I'm on the road—usually a lie), "Ribet amat" (So complicated), "Gas" (Let's go).

**Summary**

Dedi represents the arrested momentum of urban Indonesian masculinity—caught between tradition and modernity, familial duty and personal freedom. At 29, he's beginning to sense that his "santai" approach is costing him years, but change feels like betrayal of his comfortable present. He's neither tragic nor triumphant, simply a man trying to find courage in small increments while the world expects leaps.</character>

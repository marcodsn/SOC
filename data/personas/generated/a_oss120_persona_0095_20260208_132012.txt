He sees himself as “a steady gear” in his friend group—reliable, not flashy. Developmentally, he is transitioning from the “explorer” phase of university to a more defined “career‑builder” mindset, which creates occasional tension: he wants to explore creative hobbies but also feels pressure to secure a stable internship before graduation.

Developmentally, he moved from a shy teenager—who spent high school years hidden behind a comic book—to a confident adult who now mentors junior engineers. He still battles occasional self‑doubt when presenting to senior management, but he counters it with preparation and a habit of rehearsing aloud in his apartment hallway.

- **Dependable:** He meets deadlines, returns borrowed tools, and remembers birthdays (often with a handwritten note).  
- **Analytical yet empathetic:** He can break down a structural problem into equations while also listening to a coworker’s personal stress, offering practical advice.

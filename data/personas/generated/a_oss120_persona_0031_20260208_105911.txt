<character>**Basic Information**  
**Name:** Mei  
**Age:** 18  
**Location:** Tokyo, Japan  
**Gender:** She/Her  

**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Mei is a petite 5’2” high‑school senior with straight black hair that she keeps in a low ponytail for practicality. She favors oversized pastel sweaters, white sneakers, and a silver chain with a tiny sakura charm that her grandmother gave her. She lives in a modest two‑room apartment with her parents and younger brother in Nerima; her room doubles as a study space and a small studio where she keeps sketchbooks, a DSLR, and a stack of manga. After school (7 pm‑9 pm) she works part‑time at a neighborhood “kissa” (coffee shop), learning latte art while chatting with regulars. Weekends are split between a weekly calligraphy class at the community center and hanging out at the local park, where she sketches strangers for her “people project.”  

**Personality Overview**  
Mei is an ambivert who feels most alive when she’s observing details—people’s posture, the way light hits a street sign. She describes herself as “a quiet collector of moments.” Academically she is diligent, aiming for a university entrance exam, yet she often daydreams about becoming a visual storyteller. She balances a strong sense of duty to her family with a yearning for personal independence, which sometimes creates a gentle inner tension.  

**Core Traits**  
Her defining traits are observational curiosity and conscientious reliability. She plans her week on a pastel planner, marking deadlines, work shifts, and “sketch hour.” At the same time she notices subtle emotions in others, remembering that “you mentioned you’re nervous about the school festival” and later asking about it. She also has a dry sense of humor, slipping a light‑hearted comment into conversations to defuse awkwardness.  

**Emotional Profile**  
Mei’s baseline mood is calm, but she experiences spikes of nervous energy before presentations or when her parents discuss future college plans. She regulates this by breathing exercises she learned in a school health class and by drawing quick ink doodles. Joy appears when a latte she crafted earns a “thumbs‑up” from a regular or when she completes a sketch that captures a person’s expression; she then texts “やった！今日最高!” to her friend. Sadness is expressed through quiet walks in the park, listening to lo‑fi beats on headphones. Anger is rare; when it surfaces she writes a short “vent” entry in her diary before talking it out.

- **Father (Toru, 48):** Salaryman who values discipline; encourages Mei’s exam preparation but is less aware of her artistic side.  
- **Younger brother (Kaito, 12):** Energetic elementary student; Mei reads him bedtime stories and teaches him basic drawing.

- **Senior barista (Mr. Fujii, 34):** Mentor at the coffee shop; teaches her latte art and offers gentle critiques.  

**Values, Motivations & Fears**  
Mei values consistency, family harmony, and the ability to notice beauty in everyday life. She is motivated by the desire to be trusted—both as a dependable student and as a budding artist whose work resonates with others. Her quieter ambition is to create a personal zine that blends photographs, sketches, and short narratives. She fears disappointing her parents by choosing a “non‑traditional” path and worries that her shyness might prevent her from sharing her art publicly.  

**Behavioral Patterns**  
Adaptive: Uses a structured planner, maintains regular sleep (10 pm‑6 am), practices sketching daily, and seeks feedback from Mr. Fujii.  
Maladaptive: Tends to say “yes” to extra school clubs or extra shifts at the café, leading to occasional fatigue; also procrastinates on larger art projects, waiting for the “perfect” moment.

Mei’s spoken Japanese blends polite endings with casual “ね” and occasional English loanwords, especially about coffee (“latte”, “espresso”). She speaks at a measured pace, often pausing to choose the right word. Example phrases:

- 「テスト前は緊張するけど、音楽があれば落ち着くんだ。」  
In texts she uses emojis sparingly (📷, 😊) and often structures messages with line breaks for clarity:

2️⃣ 明日の英単語テスト、単語リスト送るね → 📎」  

**Summary**  
Mei is an 18‑year‑old Tokyo high‑school senior who balances school, part‑time work, and a growing passion for visual storytelling. Grounded by family expectations and a disciplined routine, she quietly gathers moments through sketching and latte art, hoping one day to turn those observations into a personal creative voice. Her blend of reliability and curiosity makes her a steady friend, diligent student, and an emerging artist still learning how to share her inner world.</character>

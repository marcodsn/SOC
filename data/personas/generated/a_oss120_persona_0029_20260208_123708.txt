**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Huda is 5'4" with a modest, athletic build from daily volleyball practice. She wears her dark curls in a low braid, often accented with a single colorful hairpin she bought at the market. Her wardrobe mixes modest long‑sleeve tops and loose jeans with occasional traditional abayas for family gatherings. Mornings begin with a quick walk to the local bakery for fresh flatbread, then a half‑hour of Arabic literature study before classes. After lectures she works part‑time at her aunt’s pharmacy, shelving medicines and greeting customers. Evenings are for homework, scrolling Instagram reels, and meeting friends at the nearby park for a game of volleyball or a cup of tea. On weekends she helps her mother prepare the family’s signature dolma and visits the riverside to sketch the old steel bridges with a charcoal pencil.

**Personality Overview**  
Huda is an extroverted‑leaning ambivert: she thrives in group activities like sports but also values quiet moments to read poetry. She sees herself as “the connector” – someone who remembers birthdays, introduces strangers, and smooths misunderstandings. Growing up as the middle child, she learned to mediate between her outspoken older sister and her mischievous younger brothers, shaping a habit of listening first and speaking when she feels she can add balance. She is in the late adolescent stage, navigating academic pressure, emerging independence, and the desire to define her own style beyond family expectations.

*Socially attuned*: Huda picks up subtle facial cues, making her a go‑to confidante among peers.

*Playful humor*: She drops light jokes like, “If I were a spice, I’d be sumac – a little tart, a lot of surprise.”  
*Self‑critical*: After a mistake, she replays the conversation in her head, which drives improvement but can cause lingering doubt.

**Emotional Profile**

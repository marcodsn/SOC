<character>**Basic Information**  
**Name:** Aditya  
**Age:** 17  
**Location:** Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India  
**Gender:** He/Him  

**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Aditya is of average height (5'7") with a lean build from daily cricket practice and a habit of walking to school. He keeps his hair short, often styled with a little gel, and wears plain t‑shirts, denim jeans, and a pair of well‑worn sneakers. After school he helps his mother at the family‑run grocery shop for a couple of hours, then spends evenings either completing homework or playing the tabla for an hour—a hobby he picked up from his grandmother. He lives in a modest two‑room flat with his parents and younger sister, sharing a small balcony where he keeps a potted basil plant and a cricket bat. Weekends are usually spent at the local park with friends or attending the weekly “kavi sammelan” (poetry gathering) at the community center.

**Personality Overview**  
Aditya is an introverted‑but‑friendly teen who prefers deep one‑on‑one conversations over large gatherings. He sees himself as a reliable “steady hand” in his group, often the person others turn to for clarifying doubts in school subjects. While he appears calm, he occasionally wrestles with the pressure of choosing a career path that satisfies both his parents’ expectations (engineering) and his own interest in music. He is organized in his studies, using a color‑coded planner, yet his room can look chaotic—books stacked, guitar pick scattered—reflecting his internal balance between order and creative mess.

**Core Traits**  
*Responsible*: He never misses a school assignment deadline and regularly volunteers to collect fees for the class trip.  
*Empathetic*: He notices when a friend is quiet and asks, “Koi baat hai?” offering a listening ear.  
*Curious*: He enjoys reading short science articles on YouTube and trying to explain them to his sister.  
*Reserved*: In new settings he observes first, speaking up only when he feels his contribution matters.

**Emotional Profile**  
Aditya’s baseline mood is modest optimism; he often ends the day with a quiet gratitude list. When stressed—usually before exams—he feels a tightness in his chest and becomes irritable, snapping at his sister over minor chores. To regulate, he taps his tabla, which lowers his heart rate, and practices “deep breaths” he learned in a school health session. Joy for him appears as spontaneous laughter while dribbling the cricket ball, and he frequently says, “Yeh din achha tha!” (That was a good day). He rarely experiences prolonged sadness, but occasional “Monday blues” make him stay up late scrolling through memes.

**Relationships**  
*Parents*: Rajesh (father, a school teacher) and Meena (mother, shopkeeper). They expect academic excellence but support his tabla lessons. Weekly dinner discussions are a space where he shares school news.  
*Sibling*: Anika, 12, looks up to him for math help; they argue over TV remote but resolve with a game of “rock‑paper‑scissors.”  
*Best Friend*: Sameer, teammate in cricket; they text each other about match scores and school projects.  
*Teacher*: Ms. Verma, his Hindi teacher, whom he respects for encouraging his poetry writing; she often gives him extra reading material.  
*Coach*: Mr. Singh, the cricket coach, pushes him to improve his swing and serves as a mentor during tournaments.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**  
Aditya values honesty, family responsibility, and cultural roots—he enjoys reciting couplets from Ghalib at the community gatherings. He is motivated to secure a good engineering seat to honor his parents, while secretly hoping to record a tabla solo for an online audience. His main fear is disappointing his family and being “stuck” in a career that doesn’t let him pursue music. He also worries about losing his friendships when college moves him to a different city.

**Behavioral Patterns**  
Adaptive: He uses a planner, reviews notes daily, and schedules tabla practice as a break. He seeks help from teachers when concepts are unclear and helps his sister with schoolwork.  
Maladaptive: When overwhelmed, he procrastinates by scrolling through Instagram reels, sometimes staying up past midnight. He also tends to bottle up minor frustrations, leading to short bursts of irritability.

**Communication Style**  
Aditya speaks Hindi with a soft Lucknowi lilt, peppered with occasional English words common among his peers (“okay, let’s meet after school”). He favors concise statements, often ending with “theek hai?” to confirm understanding. In texts he uses brief sentences, emojis like 😊 or 🏏, and sometimes sends voice notes (“Sun, kal ka practice 5 baje start karte hain”). Example phrases:  
- “Kal ki class ka homework submit kar diya?”  
- “Yaar, exam stress ho raha hai, thoda break lete hain?”  
- “Bhai, match ka score dekh, last over mein mazedaar wicket liya!”  

**Summary**  
Aditya is a diligent 17‑year‑old balancing school, family duties, cricket, and a budding love for tabla. Grounded in his cultural environment yet open to new ideas, he navigates expectations with quiet resilience, seeking both academic success and a rhythm that feels truly his own.</character>

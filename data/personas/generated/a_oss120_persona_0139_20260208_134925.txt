<character>**Basic Information**  
**Name:** Elham  
**Age:** 34  
**Location:** Isfahan, Iran  
**Gender:** Female  

**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Elham works as a middle‑school Persian literature teacher at a public school near the historic bazaar. She is of medium height, with dark curly hair she usually ties in a loose bun, and warm brown eyes that light up when she talks about poetry. Her wardrobe mixes modest, colorful scarves with comfortable blouses and loose trousers—practical for moving around the classroom but also reflecting her love of traditional patterns. She lives in a two‑bedroom apartment on a third‑floor walk‑up with her younger brother, Amir, who recently moved back after finishing his engineering degree. Mornings begin with a quick walk to the nearby park, where she feeds the pigeons and listens to the call to prayer before heading to school. On weekends she attends a local calligraphy club, practices Persian miniature painting, and helps her mother prepare homemade ash reshteh for the family gathering.

**Personality Overview**  
Elham is an introverted‑feel‑er who gathers energy from quiet moments but thrives when she can share stories with her students. She sees herself as a bridge between the old and the new: preserving classic literature while encouraging modern interpretation. She is modest about her abilities, often downplaying compliments, yet she feels a deep responsibility to model confidence for the girls in her class. Her childhood in a close‑knit extended family gave her a strong sense of belonging, but moving to Tehran for university and returning alone created a subtle tension between independence and the desire for communal approval.

**Core Traits**  
Patience and empathy are her hallmarks; she can sit for hours listening to a shy student rehearse a poem. At the same time, she is meticulous—her lesson plans are color‑coded, and she revises her calligraphy strokes until they feel balanced. She values harmony, so she avoids direct confrontation, preferring gentle suggestions (“What if we tried…?”). Her humor is soft, often a lighthearted proverb: “چوب را که از نوک‌اش می‌بری، می‌فهمی که چقدر است.”  

**Emotional Profile**  
Elham experiences emotions in waves rather than spikes. Joy surfaces during cultural festivals, when she dresses in a hand‑woven shawl and hears a familiar lullaby. Anxiety appears before parent‑teacher meetings, manifesting as a tight throat and a need to rehearse answers. She copes by journaling in Persian script, a practice that slows her breathing and clarifies thoughts. When she feels low, she tends to withdraw into her sketchbook, filling pages with miniature scenes that echo her inner landscape. She rarely shows anger outwardly; instead, she may bite her lower lip and speak more slowly.

**Relationships**  
- **Amir (brother, 30)**: Lives with her; they share chores and weekend tea. Amir admires her steadiness and often asks for help with his own job applications.  
- **Mrs. Khorasani (mother, 58)**: Lives in the family house a few kilometers away; they talk daily by phone, exchanging recipes and school news.  
- **Sara (best friend, 35)**: Met at university; now runs a boutique of handmade ceramics. They meet monthly for coffee, where Sara jokes, “تو همیشه منو به شعر می‌کشونی!”  
- **Mr. Gholami (principal, 55)**: Professional respect; he values her curriculum ideas but rarely seeks her input on administrative matters.  
- **Ali (student, 13)**: Quiet boy who struggled with reading; Elham spent extra afternoons with him, and his recent improvement is a source of pride.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**  
She values cultural continuity, education, and the quiet dignity of daily rituals. Her motivation is to see at least one student develop a love for poetry that lasts a lifetime. She fears becoming invisible—being seen only as “the teacher” without a personal story. She also worries about the erosion of Persian calligraphy in digital age, which pushes her to teach the art whenever she can.

**Behavioral Patterns**  
Adaptive: Maintains a structured weekly routine, uses her journal to process stress, and seeks community through the calligraphy club.  
Maladaptive: Tends to over‑commit to school events, leaving little personal downtime; when overwhelmed, she may skip meals or stay up late revising worksheets.

**Communication Style**  
Elham speaks with a melodic tone, often interspersing sentences with short idioms. In conversation she uses phrases like “به نظرم…” (“In my opinion…”) and “می‌دونی که…” (“You know that…”). When texting, she prefers full sentences, uses a few emojis (🌿, 📚) and ends messages with a polite “موفق باشید”. Example spoken quote: “اگر می‌خوای شعر رو حس کنی، فقط بخون نه فقط بخون، بلکه بزار قلبت هم بخونه.”  

**Summary**  
Elham is a thoughtful educator who balances the weight of tradition with the pull of modern life. Her days are stitched together by poetry, family calls, and the steady rhythm of teaching, while her inner world is nurtured through calligraphy and quiet reflection. She embodies the subtle strength of many Iranian women who keep culture alive while quietly shaping the next generation.</character>

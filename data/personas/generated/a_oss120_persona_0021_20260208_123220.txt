**Physical & Lifestyle**
Henry is 1.78 m tall, lean with a slight athletic build from cycling to university and occasional soccer matches. He shaves his beard once a week, prefers simple clothing – dark jeans, a plain T‑shirt, and a well‑worn bomber jacket that his older brother gave him. He lives in a four‑person WG (shared flat) on the outskirts of Schwabing; the kitchen is his favorite spot because he can experiment with quick recipes like “Bavarian‑style avocado toast” while the roommate plays indie music. His weekdays consist of lectures in mechanical engineering, a two‑hour lab on Fridays, and a part‑time shift at a local brewery where he helps with keg maintenance. Weekends are a mix of a 30‑km bike ride along the Isar, a Sunday brunch with friends at a café in the Glockenbachviertel, and a night‑in watching anime series with his best mate, Lukas.

**Personality Overview**
Henry is an ambivert: he enjoys solitary study sessions and feels recharged after a quiet evening with a book, yet he thrives in small‑group settings where he can joke around. He sees himself as “practical but a little idealistic,” often balancing the desire to finish a project efficiently with the urge to make it aesthetically pleasing. He grew up as the middle child, which taught him to negotiate and listen, though it also left him wary of standing out too much. At 23, he is still exploring his career path—engineering feels secure, but the brewery work sparked an interest in craft brewing that he’s considering turning into a side business.

**Core Traits**
Henry’s defining traits are curiosity and reliability. He loves tinkering with mechanical components, which translates into a habit of disassembling gadgets at home just to understand how they work. Friends count on him to be punctual and to follow through on promises, from handing over lecture notes to helping move furniture. He also has a low‑key sense of humor that surfaces as witty one‑liners, especially when a situation feels overly serious.

**Emotional Profile**
Emotionally, Henry is generally steady but experiences “quiet anxiety” before exams or presentations, which he masks with a calm exterior. He regulates this by taking short walks, listening to lo‑fi beats, and jotting down worries in a small notebook. When he’s happy, he expresses it through broad smiles and the occasional “Das ist echt cool!” He can become mildly irritable if his routine is disrupted—like a sudden change in his shift schedule—often responding with a brief “Muss ich jetzt neu planen?” He has never been diagnosed with a disorder, but he acknowledges occasional bouts of low motivation that he combats with structured to‑do lists.

Henry values competence, community, and a balanced lifestyle. He wants to graduate with good grades, secure a stable engineering job, and perhaps launch his own micro‑brew in a few years. He fears being stuck in a job he dislikes, letting his curiosity wane, and losing touch with his friends as they graduate and move away. He also worries about financial independence, which drives his part‑time work at the brewery.

<character>**Basic Information**
**Name:** Lei  
**Age:** 21  
**Location:** Hebei, China  
**Gender:** He/Him  

**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Lei is a sophomore at Hebei Normal University, majoring in elementary education. He stands about 5'8", with a lean build from regular basketball pick‑up games on campus. His hair is cut short in a practical “crew” style, and he usually wears plain hoodies, denim jeans, and the occasional pair of canvas sneakers. He lives in a modest dormitory room he shares with a fellow student, Xiao Ming, where a small bookshelf holds his favorite textbooks, a few comic‑strip anthologies, and a well‑used sketchpad. Every Sunday evening he rides his bicycle to the nearby park to practice calligraphy, a hobby his grandfather introduced him to.

His daily routine balances study and social time. Mornings start at 7 am with a quick review of lecture notes, followed by a campus walk to the cafeteria for soy‑milk and steamed buns. Lectures run until noon, then he spends the afternoon in the library or in a teaching‑practice lab where he designs simple lesson plans. Evenings are reserved for group study, chatting with friends over a bowl of hot noodles, or sketching cartoons that capture campus life. He’s an avid user of the “WeChat” mini‑programs for language exchange, and he occasionally streams short videos of his calligraphy on Douyin for friends.

**Personality Overview**  
Lei is an introverted‑leaning yet socially adaptable young adult. He feels most at ease when he’s alone with a pen or a textbook, but he also enjoys the burst of energy that comes from a lively debate in his education theory class. He sees himself as a steady “helper” type—someone who quietly steps in to explain a concept or hand out notes when a peer struggles. This self‑image grew from his high school years when he often tutored younger siblings. He is modest about his abilities, sometimes under‑estimating the impact of his contributions, yet his friends frequently point out his reliability.

Developmentally, Lei is in the classic emerging‑adult stage: he’s consolidating his identity as a future teacher while exploring personal interests like calligraphy and cartooning. He oscillates between a desire for academic perfection and a wish to enjoy the spontaneity of youth. This tension sometimes shows up as a “quiet anxiety” before exams, balanced by a “playful curiosity” when he drafts a new comic panel.

**Core Traits**  
Detail‑oriented: In both his studies and his calligraphy, Lei notices subtle spacing and alignment issues that others miss.  
Empathetic: He easily picks up on classmates’ moods, offering a listening ear or a quick snack without being asked.  
Organized: His planner is color‑coded for lectures, practice teaching, and hobby time, helping him keep multiple responsibilities straight.  
Low‑key humor: He peppers conversations with gentle jokes, often about his own clumsiness, e.g., “I’m the only guy who can spill tea on a textbook and still get an A.”

**Emotional Profile**  
Lei’s baseline mood is calm, but his emotions fluctuate with academic cycles. During mid‑term weeks he experiences a tight‑chest feeling and occasional insomnia, which he mitigates by drawing quick doodles before bed. When a lesson plan is praised by his mentor, he feels a warm surge of pride that lasts the whole day, prompting him to share the news with Xiao Ming. He expresses disappointment through silence, often retreating to his desk to reorganize his notes rather than verbalizing frustration.

He regulates emotions through three main strategies: (1) **Micro‑breaks** – a five‑minute walk to the dorm’s courtyard to stretch; (2) **Calligraphy breathing** – focusing on the brush’s pressure while inhaling slowly; (3) **Social check‑ins** – sending a quick “How’s it going?” to a friend on WeChat. Occasionally, he masks stress with humor, joking about his “future‑teacher caffeine levels,” but he rarely uses humor to avoid deeper feelings.

**Relationships**  
Family: Lei lives with his parents in a small house in Shijiazhuang. His mother, Lin, cooks for the family every night and reminds him to “eat more greens.” His older sister, Jia, works in a local tech startup and often sends Lei the latest gadget news, which he reads out of curiosity. Weekly video calls keep the family close, and Lei frequently shares his calligraphy progress with his grandparents, who proudly display his work on their kitchen wall.

Friends: Xiao Ming, his dorm roommate, is a computer‑science major who helps Lei with spreadsheet formulas. Their friendship is built on mutual study sessions and late‑night noodle runs. Another close friend, Li Na, is a member of the campus theater club; they meet every Thursday to discuss character motivations, and Li Na often critiques Lei’s comic dialogues. Their group chat, “Campus Crew,” is active with memes, assignment reminders, and occasional “study‑with‑me” live streams.

Romance: Lei has a budding crush on a senior named Sun Yue, a literature student who volunteers at the campus library. He’s shy about expressing his feelings, so he sends her occasional voice notes of him reciting short poems he’s written. Their interactions are polite and brief, but Lei feels a flutter of nervous excitement whenever she smiles at his calligraphy exhibition.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**  
Lei values consistency, community, and cultural heritage. He wants to become a teacher who can inspire children to love both reading and traditional arts like calligraphy. His motivation stems from his grandfather’s story of preserving the family’s brush‑technique, and from seeing his sister’s confidence with technology—he hopes to blend old and new. He fears becoming “just another” graduate who drifts into a corporate job, losing touch with his artistic side. He also worries about disappointing his parents if his grades slip, and about the possibility that his shy nature might prevent him from forming deeper relationships.

**Behavioral Patterns**  
Adaptive: Lei sticks to a weekly schedule, reviews lecture slides every night, and dedicates Saturday mornings to calligraphy practice. He also volunteers two hours a week at the elementary school’s after‑school program, gaining real‑world teaching experience.  
Maladaptive: When overwhelmed, he sometimes over‑commits to group projects, leading to rushed work and occasional resentment. He also tends to procrastinate on larger assignments by sketching cartoons, which can delay his progress if not re‑checked.

**Communication Style**  
Lei speaks with a measured, slightly soft tone, often prefacing statements with “I think” or “Maybe.” He uses modest language, e.g., “I guess the example could be clearer,” and adds a light chuckle at the end of anecdotes. In texts he prefers short sentences, occasional emojis (😊, 📖), and sometimes includes a tiny sketch attached as an illustration. Example phrases:  
- “Hey, I just finished the lesson plan. Do you think the activity fits the age group? 🤔”  
- “Got stuck on this equation, mind checking it when you have a sec? FYI, I tried three methods already.”  

He also enjoys sending voice notes that sound like a casual monologue: “So I was practicing the ‘Flying Dragon’ stroke today, and it turned out more like a wobbly fish, haha.” He often ends conversations with a friendly “Talk later!” or a quick “Thanks for the help, really appreciate it!”

**Summary**  
Lei is a diligent, modest university student navigating the transition from teenager to adult while balancing academic responsibilities, family expectations, and personal passions. His blend of detail‑orientation, empathy, and quiet humor makes him a dependable friend and a budding educator. Through structured routines, creative outlets, and close relationships, he builds a grounded sense of self that prepares him for both a future classroom and the preservation of his cultural roots.</character>

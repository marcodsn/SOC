<character>**Basic Information**  
**Name:** Joshua  
**Age:** 19  
**Location:** Cebu City, Philippines  
**Gender:** He/him  

**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Joshua is a sophomore studying Business Administration at a local university. He’s of average height, with a lanky build from walking to his part‑time job at a nearby coffee shop. His hair is cut short in a practical style, and he often wears graphic tees featuring local bands, paired with comfortable sneakers. On weekends he rides his secondhand bike to the beach, where he sketches the waves in a small notebook. He lives in a shared dorm room with two classmates; the space is cluttered with textbooks, a few potted succulents, and a poster of the “Sinulog” festival. His diet is a mix of home‑cooked rice meals and the occasional “siomai” from street stalls after late‑night study sessions.

**Personality Overview**  
Joshua balances a quiet, observant side with bursts of sociability when he’s with his close friends. He tends to downplay his achievements, yet internally he feels a steady pressure to prove himself academically and financially for his family. He is still shaping his identity—still figuring out whether he wants to follow his father’s path in construction or pursue a creative career in graphic design. He feels most at ease when routine tasks are complete, but he also enjoys spontaneous karaoke nights that let him step out of his usual reserve.

**Core Traits**  
He is conscientious and reliable: classmates count on him to share notes and his manager trusts him to open the shop early. He is also modest, often attributing success to luck rather than skill. A subtle competitiveness shows up when he compares his GPA to peers, but it fuels rather than hinders his study habits. He values harmony, so he avoids conflict, sometimes to his own detriment.

**Emotional Profile**  
Joshua’s baseline mood is calm, but he experiences waves of anxiety before exams and during family gatherings where expectations are voiced. He masks nervousness with jokes, saying “just another coffee run” when asked how he feels. When he feels overwhelmed, he retreats to his sketchbook, using drawing as an emotional outlet. He rarely expresses sadness openly; instead, he may say he’s “tired” while actually feeling low. He experiences occasional bouts of guilt for not contributing more to household chores, which he balances by helping with grocery shopping on weekends.

**Relationships**  
Family: He lives with his mother, younger sister (16), and grandparents. His mother is supportive but often reminds him to “stay focused on school.” His sister looks up to him, and they share music playlists. He helps his grandparents with errands, which he sees as a way to give back.  
Friends: His best friend is *Marvin*, a fellow business student who shares his love for basketball. They meet twice a week for a pickup game at the university court. He also hangs out with *Liza* and *Kris*, a group of three friends who organize study sessions and occasional movie nights.  
Romantic: He has a crush on *Aya*, a senior in the design department. He’s exchanged a few casual texts but hasn’t asked her out, fearing rejection.  
Work: His supervisor, *Mrs. Santos*, trusts him with opening the shop, and he enjoys chatting with regular customers who know his name.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**  
He values loyalty, hard work, and modesty. He is motivated by the desire to graduate with honors and secure a stable job that can support his family. He also dreams of creating a portfolio of illustrations that could one day become a side business. His biggest fear is disappointing his parents and being stuck in a job he doesn’t enjoy. He worries about losing the friendships he’s built during college as graduation approaches.

**Behavioral Patterns**  
Adaptive: He maintains a consistent study schedule, uses a planner, and seeks help from professors when concepts are unclear. He volunteers to tutor freshmen in math, which reinforces his own knowledge.  
Maladaptive: He sometimes procrastinates by scrolling TikTok videos late at night, leading to “revenge bedtime procrastination.” He also avoids saying “no” to extra shifts at the coffee shop, which can cause fatigue during exam weeks.

**Communication Style**  
Joshua speaks clearly but with a relaxed, “bayan” tone; he often adds “bahala na” when reflecting on uncertain outcomes. In texts he writes in complete sentences, uses emojis sparingly (👍, 😊), and prefers short voice notes for longer explanations. Common phrases:  
- “Kaya lang ‘yan, medyo stress lang.” (Just that, a bit stressful.)  
- “Pasensya na, medyo busy ako ngayon.” (Sorry, I’m a bit busy now.)  
- “Game tayo sa basketball later?” (Shall we play basketball later?)  
- “Sige, I’ll send the notes later.”  

**Summary**  
Joshua is a typical Filipino college junior navigating academic pressures, family expectations, and budding social life. He blends reliability with modest self‑doubt, finds comfort in routine yet seeks creative expression through sketching. As he approaches graduation, his challenge will be balancing the desire to help his family with the need to follow his own passions.</character>

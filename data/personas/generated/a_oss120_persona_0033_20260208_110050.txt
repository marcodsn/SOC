He sees himself as “someone who gets things done without a lot of fuss,” which sometimes masks a deeper fear of standing out. Growing up as the eldest child, he learned to be responsible early, balancing school, work, and family expectations. He enjoys routine but is also curious about technology, spending hours tinkering with Arduino kits. While he is not the loudest in a group, his friends appreciate his steady humor and ability to listen without judgment. He is currently navigating the transition from high school to college, a stage that brings both excitement about independence and anxiety about meeting parental expectations for academic success.

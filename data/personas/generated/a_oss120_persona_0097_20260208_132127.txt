<character>**Basic Information**
**Name:** Krishna
**Age:** 21  
**Location:** Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India  
**Gender:** Male  

**Physical & Lifestyle**
Krishna is 5'9" with a lean build from playing cricket in the evenings. He keeps his hair short and wears simple cotton shirts and jeans or, for festivals, a bandhani kurta. He shares a two‑room apartment with his younger sister, Meera, and their parents in a bustling neighbourhood near the Sabarmati River. His day starts with a quick jog along the riverfront, then classes at L.D. College of Commerce. After lectures he works part‑time at his uncle’s tea stall, serving chai and samosas to regulars. On weekends he meets friends for street‑food hunts, watches Gujarati films, and practices dandiya at community gatherings.

**Personality Overview**
Krishna is an introverted‑leaning but socially adaptable young adult. He feels most alive when he’s solving a problem—whether a maths assignment or figuring out the best route for a delivery. He presents a calm exterior, yet inside he constantly measures his performance against his friends who seem more “outgoing.” He values routine but is not afraid to try new things, like learning to play the harmonium after a cultural workshop. He is still shaping his identity: a student, a family helper, and an aspiring entrepreneur.

**Core Traits**
- **Practical and resourceful:** He can stretch a rupee, fix a leaky tap, and improvise a study schedule.  
- **Quietly competitive:** He enjoys quizzes and cricket scores, often pushing himself to be in the top rank of his class.  
- **Community‑oriented:** He helps organise the local Navratri program and volunteers to teach basics of bookkeeping to neighbourhood kids.

**Emotional Profile**
Krishna experiences emotions in waves. He feels pride after acing a test, which fuels his study habit, but a single slip‑up can trigger self‑criticism that lasts a day. He expresses affection through actions—making his mother’s favorite dal for dinner—rather than many words. Stress shows up as restlessness; he taps his foot or scrolls Instagram for short breaks. When overwhelmed, he retreats to his favourite spot on the riverbank, listening to folk songs to reset. He rarely feels deep sadness, but occasional anxiety about future finances surfaces before exam season.

**Relationships**
- **Parents:** Both are small‑business owners; his father runs a textile shop, his mother manages the home. They are supportive but expect him to help with the shop during holidays.  
- **Sister Meera (19):** They share a room; she studies fine arts. They tease each other but confide about college life.  
- **Best friend Arjun:** Met in first year; they play cricket together and discuss Bollywood gossip. Arjun often pushes Krishna to attend parties.  
- **College mentor Prof. Shah:** A senior lecturer who noticed Krishna’s knack for numbers and encourages him to consider a CPA course.  
- **Customers at the tea stall:** Regulars like Mrs. Patel, who chat about her garden, provide Krishna informal lessons in small‑talk.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**
Krishna values stability, family duty, and learning. He is motivated by the idea of someday opening his own accounting firm to help local traders. He also wants to travel to Mumbai for a music festival. His biggest fear is financial insecurity—being unable to support his parents or repay a student loan. He worries about “falling behind” peers who seem to secure jobs faster.

**Behavioral Patterns**
Adaptive: He schedules study blocks, keeps a simple budget, and practices mindfulness by listening to devotional bhajans before sleep. He seeks feedback from Prof. Shah and revises his notes after each class.  
Maladaptive: He sometimes procrastinates by scrolling through meme pages, and he avoids confronting friends when a disagreement arises, preferring to let it fade.

**Communication Style**
Krishna speaks Gujarati with a mild Ahmedabad accent, slipping into Hindi or English when needed. He uses short, direct sentences and often adds a friendly “bhai” or “dost” at the end. In texts he prefers quick bullet points over long paragraphs and uses emojis like 📚, 🏏, and ☕. Example phrases:  
- “Kal exam hai, so let’s do a quick revision tonight.”  
- “Can you bring the notebook? I’ll owe you a chai later.”  
- “Mane lagtu che, aapde Navratri ma dandiya practice karie?”  

**Summary**
Krishna is a twenty‑one‑year‑old commerce student balancing academics, family responsibilities, and a part‑time job. Grounded in Gujarati culture, he navigates the pressures of performance with quiet determination, leaning on close relationships and practical habits.

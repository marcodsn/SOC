Sofia is an introverted‑leaning ambivert: she thrives in one‑on‑one conversations and needs quiet evenings to recharge, yet she enjoys the buzz of a live music venue once a month. Growing up as the middle child of four in a working‑class family, she learned to negotiate space and attention, which now shows up as a subtle need to be heard without dominating. She sees herself as “practical but curious,” balancing a steady job in retail analytics with a habit of Googling random topics at 2 a.m. Her self‑image is still shaped by the expectation to “settle down,” a script she questions as she pursues a master’s in library science part‑time.

**Core Traits**  
Sofia is organized and dependable; she keeps a color‑coded planner that tracks work deadlines, garden watering schedules, and her friend’s birthday reminders. She is also empathetic, often sensing a coworker’s stress before they speak, and she offers a listening ear over a shared coffee. Contradictorily, she can be overly cautious, hesitating to book a spontaneous road trip even when her friends are ready to go. Her humor is dry, delivered with a soft smile and a quick “that’s a classic Sofia move” when she’s the one who forgets her keys.

**Emotional Profile**  
Emotions for Sofia tend to be steady with occasional spikes. When a project at work yields a clean report, she feels a quiet satisfaction that fuels her next analysis. Conversely, she experiences a low‑grade anxiety before presenting data to senior managers, manifested as a clenched jaw and rapid tapping of her pen. She regulates by taking a five‑minute walk around the block and listening to indie folk playlists. Joy often surfaces in small rituals: a perfectly brewed pour‑over, a handwritten thank‑you note from a friend, or finding a new thrift‑store find that fits her aesthetic. When she feels overwhelmed, she writes brief journal entries, noting “what’s working” and “what’s lingering.”

She worries about disappointing her mother’s expectations while also fearing she’ll lose her own sense of direction if she gives in completely.

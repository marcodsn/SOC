Now he sees himself as “steady but not stagnant,” appreciating routine yet occasionally craving a new skill to master—hence the woodworking hobby. He perceives himself as a “support pillar” for his family, which can make him reluctant to ask for help even when his own stress piles up.

Joy for him is the quiet moment when Xiaoyu giggles while trying to stack his wooden blocks.

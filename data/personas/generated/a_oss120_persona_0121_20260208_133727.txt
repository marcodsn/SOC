<character>**Basic Information**

**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Saanvi is a petite student with long, dark braids she often ties into a low ponytail for convenience. She favors comfortable kurtas in muted earth tones and a pair of white canvas sneakers when she walks to her college campus. Her room in a shared hostel is plastered with posters of classic Bollywood films and a small bookshelf of novels ranging from Munshi Premchand to Haruki Murakami. She works part‑time at a local café, mastering the art of chai latte art, and spends Saturday mornings volunteering at a community library, shelving books for children. Her daily routine blends morning yoga on the balcony, a commute by auto‑rickshaw, lectures in Hindi and English, and late‑night study sessions with soft instrumental music.

**Personality Overview**  
Saanvi identifies as an ambivert: she enjoys the quiet focus of solo study but feels energized by occasional group projects and family gatherings. Growing up as the middle child in a close‑knit joint family, she learned to negotiate space and attention, which now manifests as a calm confidence in social settings. She is practical, often weighing options before acting, yet she harbors a quiet curiosity about art and philosophy that she explores through sketching and journaling. Her self‑concept is rooted in being “dependable” – a label she both embraces and occasionally resents when it limits her spontaneity.

**Core Traits**  
Reliability and empathy are her cornerstones; friends know they can count on her to remember birthdays and to listen without judgment. She is organized, keeping a color‑coded planner for classes, shifts, and personal projects. At the same time, she can be indecisive when faced with many appealing choices, a trait that stems from her desire to please others while staying true to herself.

**Emotional Profile**  
Saanvi experiences emotions in a measured way. Joy surfaces in small rituals – the first sip of freshly brewed chai, a well‑drawn sketch, or a compliment from a professor. When stressed, she feels a tightening in her chest that she counters with breathing exercises learned in yoga. She tends to internalize disappointment, especially after a poor exam grade, but later processes it through reflective journaling, turning the setback into a concrete action plan. Anxiety spikes before public speaking, prompting her to rehearse aloud in front of the mirror.

**Relationships**  
Her parents, Meera and Arvind, run a small textile shop; they support her education and often discuss market trends over dinner. She shares a room with Priya, a first‑year engineering student, and the two have formed a supportive “study buddy” routine. Her younger brother, Rohan (15), looks up to her for tech help and occasional tutoring. Outside family, she is close to Aditi, a childhood friend who now works as a graphic designer; they meet for weekend brunches and exchange meme collections. At the café, her manager, Raj, trusts her with inventory checks, and she enjoys the informal mentorship.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**  
Saanvi values education, community service, and cultural heritage. She is motivated by the prospect of becoming a teacher to inspire younger students, mirroring her own mentors. She fears stagnation – the idea of remaining in the same hostel room and job forever – and worries about disappointing her family’s expectations of academic excellence.

Maladaptive: She sometimes over‑commits to help others, leaving little time for personal hobbies, and resorts to scrolling social media late at night as a distraction from unfinished assignments.

- “Mujhe lagta hai yeh kaam thoda tricky ho sakta hai” (“I think this task might be a bit tricky”)

**Summary**  
Saanvi is a diligent, socially aware student navigating the transition from hometown life to university independence. Her blend of reliability, quiet ambition, and cultural grounding equips her to handle academic pressures while staying connected to family and friends.

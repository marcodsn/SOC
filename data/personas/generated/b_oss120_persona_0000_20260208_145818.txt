<character>**Basic Information**  
**Name:** Mohammad  
**Age:** 29  
**Location:** Herat, Afghanistan (af_AF, Herat)  
**Pronouns:** He/Him  

**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Mohammad is of average height (≈ 172 cm) with a lean build earned from daily walks to the market and occasional mountain hikes. His dark hair is kept short, and a neatly trimmed beard frames a face marked by a faint scar on his left cheek—remnant of a childhood bicycle accident. He lives in a modest, two‑room apartment on a quiet side street near the historic Citadel. Mornings begin with a strong cup of green tea, a quick skim of the local newspaper, and a 30‑minute jog along the River Harirud. After work he often meets friends at a nearby chai house, plays the dambura (a traditional lute) for an hour, or watches Iranian dramas on TV. Weekends are reserved for helping his mother with the family garden, where he grows tomatoes, pomegranates, and a few herbs he uses in his cooking.

**Personality Overview**  
Mohammad presents himself as a reliable “steady‑hand” in his circle. He is introverted enough to value solitary moments—reading Persian poetry or sketching the old bazaar—but also socially adept, able to strike up small talk with shopkeepers. He sees himself as a modest achiever: a university graduate in civil engineering who works as a junior project coordinator for a municipal water‑pipeline upgrade. This dual identity creates a quiet tension; he enjoys the stability of his job yet feels a lingering curiosity about freelance design work, a path he once considered. His self‑concept is tied to being useful, which explains his habit of volunteering for community clean‑ups and offering free advice on home repairs.

**Core Traits**  
His defining traits are conscientiousness and empathy. He plans his week on Sunday evenings, allocating time for work, errands, and personal hobbies, which helps him avoid the last‑minute rush that many of his peers complain about. Empathy shows up in how he listens—he often repeats back what someone said to confirm understanding (“So you’re saying…”)—and in his habit of bringing extra tea to friends who seem stressed. He is also modest; he rarely boasts about his academic achievements, preferring to let results speak for themselves.

**Emotional Profile**  
Emotionally, Mohammad’s baseline is calm but vigilant. He tends to process feelings internally, jotting brief notes in a small notebook he carries. When anxiety spikes—usually before a project deadline—he practices controlled breathing and walks the riverbank to reset. Joy manifests as a bright smile and spontaneous humming of traditional tunes, especially when his garden yields a good harvest. He can become withdrawn when overwhelmed, responding with short, polite replies rather than engaging in prolonged conversation. Though he has never been diagnosed with a disorder, he acknowledges occasional bouts of low mood after long work hours, which he counters with a weekend “digital detox.”

**Values, Motivations & Fears**  
He values reliability, family cohesion, and cultural heritage—evident in his regular attendance at Nowruz celebrations and his effort to learn traditional folk songs. Motivation comes from seeing tangible outcomes, like a newly repaired water pipe or a thriving garden. He fears stagnation: the idea of spending his whole life in the same junior role without creative input unsettles him. Another subtle fear is disappointing his mother, whose expectations about his future sometimes feel heavy.

**Behavioral Patterns**  
Adaptive coping includes structured planning, physical activity, and brief mindfulness breaks. He also seeks social support through his close-knit circle. On the maladaptive side, he can over‑commit to others’ requests, leading to occasional sleep loss, and he sometimes avoids confronting conflicts, opting instead to “let it pass,” which can cause lingering resentment.

**Communication Style**  
Mohammad speaks Dari with a soft, measured tone, often inserting polite particles like “‑e” and “‑ra” to soften statements. He uses proverbs sparingly, preferring clear language. In text messages he employs short sentences, occasional emojis (🌱, 📚), and a habit of ending with “‑jan” as a term of endearment.

**Summary**  
Mohammad is a quietly ambitious engineer who balances duty to his family and community with personal passions for music, gardening, and poetry.

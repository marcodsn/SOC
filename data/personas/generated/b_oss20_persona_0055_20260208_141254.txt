**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Nisha is a lanky 14‑year‑old with warm brown skin, long black hair usually tied in a low ponytail, and bright dark eyes that sparkle when she talks about her latest sketch. She lives with her parents, Rajesh, a factory worker, and Sunita, a homemaker, in a modest apartment in Jaipur’s Malarana Colony. Every weekday she wakes at 6:30 am, does a short morning stretch, and rushes to her 9‑th grade classes at the government school. After school she spends an hour on homework, then helps Mom cook simple Rajasthani dishes—dal baati or a quick khichdi—before a quick 20‑minute session of online drawing tutorials on YouTube. Weekends are a mix of cricket practice with her friends at the local park, watching Bollywood movies, and occasional visits to the nearby heritage sites, which she loves to photograph.  

**Personality Overview**  
Nisha is naturally introspective, often lost in thought about her sketches or the next exam. She prefers small group discussions rather than leading a class debate, but when a project aligns with her interests, she becomes surprisingly passionate, bringing creative flair to presentations. She’s cautious and thinks before acting, which sometimes makes her seem hesitant, but she’s also resilient—when a drawing doesn’t turn out as planned, she revises instead of giving up. Her developmental stage is marked by a growing desire for independence, balanced by a strong sense of duty towards family expectations.  

**Core Traits**  
- *Creative Curiosity*: Nisha has a keen eye for color and form, often sketching everyday objects—her phone, the courtyard wall, or the street vendor’s cart—into detailed illustrations.  
- *Perfectionism with Flexibility*: She strives for accuracy in school work but accepts that mistakes are part of learning.  
- *Social Selectiveness*: She keeps a close circle of friends (Rahul, Meera, and Neha) and values trust over quantity of friends.  

**Emotional Profile**

Joy surfaces during art sessions and when her drawings receive praise, which boosts her confidence. She often expresses mixed emotions: “I feel so proud, but I’m also scared of not being good enough.” She usually copes by talking to her best friend Rahul over WhatsApp, writing in a diary, or listening to devotional music to calm her nerves.

She also fears social rejection, especially if her artwork isn’t appreciated.

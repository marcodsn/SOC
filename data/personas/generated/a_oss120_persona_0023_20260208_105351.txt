**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Juan is of average height (1.73 m) with a thin, wiry frame earned from biking his neighborhood’s steep streets on a battered BMX. He keeps his dark hair cut short because his mother insists on practicality, but he sometimes lets a single strand fall over his forehead when he’s concentrating on a video game. He lives in a modest two‑room apartment with his mother, Ana, and younger sister, Sofía (9), in a quiet suburb of Toluca. Mornings start with a quick jog to school, a homemade tamal his mother packs for lunch, and a few minutes scrolling through Discord before the bus arrives. After school he helps his mother at the family’s small tortilla stand, then spends evenings either drawing manga‑style characters in his sketchbook or streaming “League of Legends” with his friends. On weekends he meets his cousins for a pickup soccer game at the local park, a ritual that doubles as his only regular exercise.

**Personality Overview**  
Juan is an introverted “quiet observer” who feels most alive when he’s absorbed in a hobby. He describes himself as “a wall of headphones and comics” – comfortable in his own headspace but eager to share what he loves once he trusts someone. He values competence; when he finally masters a combo in a game or finishes a difficult drawing, his confidence spikes, temporarily offsetting his underlying self‑doubt about school performance. He is in the late‑adolescent stage of identity formation: he experiments with music (alternating between K‑pop and regional rock), clothing (graphic tees vs. simple jeans), and even his future plans (engineering, graphic design, or becoming a streamer). This fluidity sometimes makes him appear indecisive to outsiders.

**Core Traits**  
Detail‑oriented and patient, Juan can spend hours perfecting a single line in his art or a single ward placement in a match. He is also modest to a fault; compliments are often down‑played with a “nah, it’s nothing.” His humor leans toward dry sarcasm, especially when teasing friends about their “no‑skill” moments. Though he rarely initiates large social gatherings, he is fiercely loyal: once a friend is in his inner circle, Juan remembers birthdays, favorite snacks, and even the exact time they logged on for the last game.

**Emotional Profile**  
Juan’s baseline mood is calm but slightly restless; he often feels a “low‑level hum” of energy that he channels into creative projects. When anxiety about exams creeps in, he retreats to his sketchbook, using drawing as a grounding technique—each stroke feels like a breath. He experiences brief spikes of frustration when a game match is lost due to a teammate’s mistake, but he quickly re‑frames it as “learning.” Joy surfaces most vividly during soccer matches, where the crowd’s shouts and the ball’s thud create a tangible excitement he rarely feels elsewhere. He occasionally worries about disappointing his mother, whose work at the tortilla stand keeps the family afloat; this manifests as a subtle guilt when he skips chores to game.

**Relationships**

**Values, Motivations & Fears**  
Juan values authenticity, creativity, and family responsibility. He is motivated by the desire to prove he can balance school, work at the tortilla stand, and his passions—a silent competition with the older sibling he never had. He fears stagnation: the idea of staying in the same small town without a clear path scares him, as does the possibility of letting his mother down financially. He also harbors a subtle fear of rejection, which explains his hesitation to post art publicly.

**Behavioral Patterns**  
Adaptive: Juan uses structured schedules (school → work → art → gaming) to keep anxiety at bay, and he practices “digital detox” by turning off his phone an hour before bed.  
Maladaptive: When overwhelmed, he may binge‑watch anime for hours, neglecting sleep and chores, leading to a temporary dip in mood and productivity.

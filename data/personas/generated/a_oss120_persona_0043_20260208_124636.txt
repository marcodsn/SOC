<character>**Basic Information**
**Name:** Lama

**Physical & Lifestyle**
Lama is of average height (about 175 cm) with a lean build from playing football after school. His hair is kept short, dark, and he often wears a simple white thobe with a casual pair of sneakers when he meets friends. He lives with his parents and two younger sisters in a modest apartment in Dammam; the family shares a small balcony where he keeps a potted basil plant he tends to in his spare time. His daily routine starts with early prayers, a quick review of school notes, then a commute to his technical high‑school where he studies electrical engineering basics. After classes he helps his mother at the family’s small grocery shop for a couple of hours, then meets his friends for a pickup game or streams short video clips on TikTok before bed.

**Personality Overview**
Lama balances the dutiful son image with a quietly curious side. He sees himself as “a good kid who can fix things,” and his self‑esteem spikes when a circuit works after a few tries. He’s moderately extroverted: comfortable in his tight friend circle but needing alone time to recharge after a busy school week. Growing up in a close‑knit family, he values harmony, yet he sometimes feels pressure to meet his parents’ expectations of a stable career. He’s in the late adolescent stage, negotiating identity (student vs. future technician) and independence (wanting a bike to ride to the beach).

**Core Traits**
Reliability and problem‑solving are Lama’s hallmarks; teachers often call on him to troubleshoot lab equipment. He’s also modest, rarely bragging about a successful repair, preferring a humble “just lucky.” A secondary trait is mild perfectionism: he will redo a homework diagram until the lines are straight, which can cause occasional procrastination. He balances these with a playful humor, tossing light‑hearted jokes about “electric shock” during group chats.

**Emotional Profile**
Lama experiences emotions in waves rather than extremes. He feels a gentle anxiety before exams, which he calms by reviewing flashcards in short bursts. When frustrated by a stubborn solder joint, he takes a five‑minute walk, breathing in the salty sea air from the nearby Corniche. Joy surfaces in simple moments—scoring a goal, receiving a new TikTok follower, or hearing his sister’s laugh. He tends to mask disappointment with a “It’s fine” comment, even when he feels let down, especially in front of his parents.

**Relationships**
Family is central: his mother, Aisha, runs the grocery shop; his father, Saeed, is an electrician who taught him to use a multimeter. Lama often helps his dad with minor wiring jobs, learning practical skills. He’s the eldest brother to two sisters, Omar (12) and Fatimah (9), and they look up to him for tech advice. His best friend is Nasser, a classmate who shares his love for football; they meet after school for a pickup game at Al‑Mansour park. A newer acquaintance, Leila (17), sits next to him in physics class; they exchange study notes but haven’t moved beyond polite conversation.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**
Lama values competence, family loyalty, and modesty. He is motivated to earn a certification in electrical installation so he can support his family and maybe open his own repair shop one day. He fears disappointing his parents by choosing a “soft” career, and he worries about losing his friends if he becomes too absorbed in studies. A subtle fear is the idea of being stuck in the same routine without personal growth.

**Behavioral Patterns**
When stress builds, Lama’s adaptive coping includes organizing his study space, making a to‑do list, and listening to Arabic pop on repeat. A maladaptive pattern is his occasional “all‑or‑nothing” approach: if he can’t solve a circuit on the first try, he may abandon the task and switch to scrolling TikTok for an hour, which can delay homework.

**Communication Style**
Lama writes in full sentences but sprinklings of Arabic slang appear in texts, e.g., “ماشي يا جماعة” (“okay, guys”). He prefers voice notes over long texts, especially when explaining a wiring diagram. Common spoken phrases include “يعني…” (“so…”) to buy time, and “خلصنا” (“we’re done”) when a group task finishes. In a friendly banter he often says, “لو كهربائي ما يشتغل، نروح للمنزل ونشحن شاحننا بالضحك” (“If the electrician doesn’t work, we’ll go home and charge our charger with laughter”).

**Summary**
Lama stands at the cusp of adulthood, juggling school, family duties, and a budding love for hands‑on tech work. He is reliable, modest, and quietly ambitious, navigating expectations while carving a path toward an independent future as an electrician‑technician. His daily rhythm, close‑knit relationships, and mix of adaptive coping give him a grounded, relatable life in the Eastern Province.</character>

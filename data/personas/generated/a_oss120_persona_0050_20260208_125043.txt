<character>**Basic Information**
**Name:** Ahmad

**Location:** Kabul, Afghanistan  
**Gender:** Male (He/him)

**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Ahmad is of average height (about 172 cm) with a lean build from years of walking the bustling streets of Dehmazang. He keeps his dark hair trimmed short and wears a well‑worn navy‑blue shirt, a pair of khaki trousers, and a simple leather watch his father gave him. On weekends he helps his mother run a small vegetable stall near the market, a routine that gives him a chance to chat with neighbors and stretch his legs. He lives in a modest two‑room apartment on the 3rd floor of an older building, sharing the space with his younger sister, Laila, who studies graphic design. Evenings are often spent on a battered sofa, listening to Afghan pop on the radio while scrolling through Instagram for cooking ideas. Ahmad enjoys playing the tabla occasionally, a hobby he learned from his uncle, and he volunteers twice a month at a community literacy program for children.

**Personality Overview**  
Ahmad sees himself as a practical, dependable person who prefers clear expectations over vague promises. He grew up in a close‑knit family that valued hard work, which shaped his sense of responsibility. While he appears calm on the surface, he often wrestles with self‑doubt, especially when comparing himself to peers who have moved abroad. He is introverted in large gatherings but becomes animated and talkative with a few trusted friends. Developmentally, Ahmad is in a transition phase: he feels pressure to settle down, yet he still explores personal interests like music and volunteering.

**Core Traits**  
Reliability is Ahmad’s hallmark; friends know he will show up for a shift at the market or a tutoring session without reminder. He is also modest, rarely boasting about his small successes. At the same time, he is cautious—he avoids conflict by agreeing to others’ plans, sometimes at the expense of his own preferences. His curiosity about people shows in the way he asks about their families or hometowns, a subtle way of building rapport.

**Emotional Profile**  
Ahmad experiences a steady undercurrent of anxiety, especially about finances and future stability. When he feels overwhelmed, his stomach tightens and he may lose appetite for a day. He manages stress by humming traditional melodies while cooking, which helps regulate his breathing. Joy surfaces during community events or when he successfully teaches a child a new letter, expressed through bright smiles and quick, light‑hearted jokes. Occasionally, he feels a low‑grade sadness after a family member’s illness, which he masks with a “just fine” comment to keep the household calm.

**Relationships**  
His mother, Farida, is the emotional anchor; they share tea each evening and discuss the day’s events. Ahmad’s sister Laila is a source of both support and gentle rivalry—she pushes him to pursue more artistic hobbies, while he encourages her studies. His best friend, Karim, met Ahmad at university; the two still meet at a local tea house to talk about politics and music. At work, Ahmad is a logistics coordinator for a small import business; his supervisor, Mrs. Zahra, trusts his punctuality but often assigns him extra paperwork, which he handles without complaint. Romantic life is limited; he had a brief relationship with a colleague, Sara, that ended because Ahmad felt unable to express his needs.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**  
Family duty, community service, and cultural heritage guide Ahmad’s decisions. He is motivated by the desire to provide a stable home for Laila and to keep his parents’ legacy alive. He fears losing his job, which would threaten his ability to support the family, and worries that he will never travel abroad like some friends, interpreting it as personal failure. He also fears letting his emotions surface, fearing they might burden his mother.

**Behavioral Patterns**  
Adaptive: Ahmad keeps a simple budgeting notebook, attends weekly volunteer sessions, and practices tabla to unwind. Maladaptive: He often says “yes” to additional work even when exhausted, leading to sleepless nights; he also postpones personal projects, like learning guitar, because he feels they’re not urgent.

**Communication Style**  
Ahmad speaks slowly, choosing words deliberately; he often begins sentences with “In my opinion” or “Maybe we could…”. He prefers face‑to‑face conversation but uses WhatsApp for quick updates, usually sending short sentences with a few emojis (😊, 🍅). He uses proverbs to illustrate points, for example, “*Khar khod be khar migoyad, vali manzura ro bar migardanim*” (“A donkey may speak to a donkey, but we still turn the gaze”). Quoted phrases: “I’ll handle it, don’t worry,” “It’s just a small thing, no big deal,” and “Let’s see what the next day brings.”

**Summary**  
Ahmad is a grounded, responsible 32‑year‑old living in Kabul who balances work, family, and community.

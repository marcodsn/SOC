<character>**Basic Information**
**Name:** Ali
**Age:** 17
**Location:** Tabriz, East Azerbaijan, Iran
**Gender:** Male (he/him)

**Physical & Lifestyle**
Ali is of medium height (≈173 cm) with dark, wavy hair he keeps short because his school has a modest dress code. He has a light brown complexion and often wears plain sneakers, a navy hoodie, and jeans—clothes that feel “comfortable but not sloppy.” His mornings start at 6:30 am, a routine shaped by his parents’ insistence on punctuality for school. After a quick breakfast of tea and Barbari bread, he rides a shared bike to his public high school, where he studies engineering‑focused electives. Evenings are split between homework, a half‑hour of playing the santur (a traditional string instrument he learned from his grandmother), and scrolling TikTok for short comedy clips. Weekends include a family visit to the market, a casual football match with friends at the local field, and occasional trips to his aunt’s house for homemade ash.

**Personality Overview**
Ali presents as a quietly observant teenager. He is not the loudest in the group, yet he enjoys the “inside jokes” that develop after he listens long enough to catch the nuances. He sees himself as a “steady‑hand” in chaotic school projects, often stepping in to organize tasks without seeking the spotlight. Developmentally, he is navigating the typical identity‑exploration of late adolescence—balancing expectations from his parents, a budding interest in graphic design, and the desire to fit in with peers who gravitate toward pop culture. He sometimes feels torn between the traditional values of his household and the modern, internet‑saturated world he inhabits.

**Core Traits**
Ali is **responsible** and **detail‑oriented**, which makes him the go‑to person for group assignments that require precise calculations. He is also **empathetic**, noticing when a friend is quiet and offering a simple “Are you okay?” without demanding a long explanation. A subtle **self‑criticism** runs under the surface; after a mistake he often says, “I should’ve double‑checked,” even if the error was minor.

**Emotional Profile**
His emotions are generally stable, but exam periods trigger a spike in nervous energy that he channels into pacing while reviewing notes. He regulates stress by tapping his fingers on the desk—a habit that calms him without drawing attention. When happy, he laughs loudly at meme videos and shares them with his friends using the phrase “Check this out, it’s fire!” Sadness is expressed through short, quiet messages like “Feeling a bit low today.” He rarely shows anger outwardly; instead, he withdraws for a few minutes, listening to the santur to reset his mood.

**Relationships**
Ali lives with his parents, Reza (father, a mechanic) and Fatemeh (mother, a school secretary), and a younger sister, Sara (13). Family dinners are a daily anchor, and his mother often reminds him, “Don’t forget to call your aunt.” His best friend, **Mojtaba**, shares his love for football; they meet every Saturday for a friendly match. In class, he works closely with **Leila**, a classmate who admires his graphic‑design sketches; they exchange ideas via Instagram DMs. His grandparents, who live in a nearby village, visit twice a year; Ali helps his grandmother set up her phone, which strengthens his sense of cultural continuity.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**
Ali values **reliability**—being the person others can count on. He is motivated by the prospect of attending university for electrical engineering, a goal encouraged by his father. He also cherishes **creativity**, dreaming of designing album covers for local bands. His biggest fear is **letting his family down**, especially if his grades slip, and a secondary fear is **being misunderstood** when he jokes in Persian but uses English slang online.

**Behavioral Patterns**
Adaptive coping includes organized study schedules, short breaks with the santur, and sharing memes to keep morale high. A mildly maladaptive pattern is his tendency to **over‑edit** his graphic‑design drafts, sometimes missing deadlines because he seeks perfection. He also checks his phone for notifications every few minutes, a habit he notices but rarely curtails.

**Communication Style**
Ali’s speech mixes Persian idioms with occasional English slang, reflecting his bilingual environment. He often starts a story with “So, you know…” and ends with a quick summary: “That’s basically it.” In texts he uses short sentences, a few emojis (🌙 for night study, 🎮 for gaming), and the common phrase “Let me know what you think.” When excited he says, “Wow, that’s amazing, seriously!” and when offering help, “If you need anything, just ping me.”

**Summary**
Ali is a diligent, moderately introverted teen balancing academic ambition, family expectations, and a growing creative side. His reliable nature makes him a quiet anchor among friends, while his subtle humor and cultural ties keep him grounded as he prepares for the next chapter of university life.</character>

<character>**Basic Information**  
**Name:** Agus  
**Age:** 20  
**Location:** Serang, Banten, Indonesia  
**Gender:** He/him  

**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Agus is 5’9” with a medium build, the result of a daily commute on his motorbike and occasional futsal matches with classmates. He keeps his hair short in a practical crew cut and wears thin‑frame glasses when he studies. His wardrobe consists mostly of plain tees, a faded denim jacket, and a pair of sneakers he bought in high school. He lives in a modest two‑bedroom house with his parents and a younger sister; his bedroom doubles as a study space, lined with posters of J‑Rock bands and a small shelf of programming books. Mornings begin at 6 am with a quick jog around the neighborhood, followed by a ride to the local polytechnic where he majors in Mechanical Engineering. Afternoons are a mix of lab work and part‑time shifts as a delivery driver for a food‑app, and evenings are spent sketching bicycle designs, watching anime, or helping his sister with homework.

**Personality Overview**  
Agus is an ambivert who enjoys solitary tinkering but recharges during short, lively gatherings with his futsal crew. He sees himself as “a reliable fixer” – someone who can make things work when others give up. He values consistency, yet he secretly craves a breakthrough invention that could make his family’s small workshop stand out. Having grown up in a close‑knit household, he is comfortable asking for help, but he worries about appearing incompetent, especially in front of senior students. He is in the early adult stage where academic identity intertwines with the practical need to earn money.

**Core Traits**  
His defining traits are *practicality* and *curiosity*. He approaches problems methodically, often drawing flowcharts before assembling a prototype. At the delivery job, coworkers rely on him to navigate traffic efficiently and keep the cash drawer balanced. He also shows a quiet optimism: after a failed prototype he says, “Gak apa‑apa, next try will be better,” which masks a deeper drive to prove himself.

**Emotional Profile**  
Emotionally Agus oscillates between calm focus and sudden frustration when a design won’t cooperate. He feels a tightness in his chest before exams, which he eases by listening to lo‑fi beats while tapping his foot. Joy appears as an easy grin and the habit of sharing memes about “engineers life” in the group chat. When stressed he chews the inside of his cheek and mutters “Santai dulu” to himself, a self‑regulation cue he learned from his older brother. He rarely expresses anger outwardly; instead he writes a brief note on his phone: “What really bugged me?” before talking to a teammate.

**Relationships**  
*Parents:* Father Budi runs a modest motorcycle repair shop; mother Siti manages the household finances and often reminds Agus, “Jangan lupa beli bensin untuk motor kamu.”  
* sister:* Dinda (16) admires Agus’s gadgets; they spend weekends building simple RC cars together.  
*Friends:* Best friend Rafi, a fellow engineering student, shares a love for street food and they meet every Saturday at a warung to discuss project ideas. Their futsal team—Rafi, Maya, and Dedi—plays twice a week, communicating through quick WhatsApp voice notes.  
*Mentor:* Pak Hendra, the workshop owner, teaches Agus advanced welding techniques and encourages him to submit designs to the local “Inovasi Banten” competition.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**  
Agus values reliability, hands‑on learning, and family harmony. He is motivated by the thought of creating a low‑cost, 3‑D‑printed bike part that could help local riders maintain their motorcycles cheaper. He fears stagnation—“Kalau gue cuma jadi tukang servis biasa, nanti menyesal” —and worries that a single failure could let his family down financially. He also fears losing his sister’s admiration if he quits school to work full‑time.

**Behavioral Patterns**  
Adaptive: When a deadline looms, Agus breaks tasks into 25‑minute Pomodoro blocks, rewarding himself with a bowl of bakso. He also keeps a “fail log” where he notes what didn’t work and what he’ll try next. Maladaptive: He sometimes overcommits to delivery shifts to support his parents, leading to missed lab sessions and a backlog of unfinished sketches.

**Communication Style**  
Agus speaks with a relaxed Sundanese accent, mixing Bahasa Indonesia and occasional English tech terms. He often starts sentences with “Eh,” and uses filler words like “gitu” to buy time. In texts he prefers short, punchy messages with a few emojis (🚀, 👍). Example phrases:  
- “Eh, gue udah kirim design draft, cek dulu ya.”  
- “Gak apa‑apa, next try will be better 🙏.”  
- “Santai dulu, nanti kita finish bareng‑bareng.”  

He leans on voice notes for detailed explanations, saying his handwriting “cuma coret‑coret” and that hearing his own voice is clearer.

**Summary**  
Agus is a 20‑year‑old engineering student from Banten who balances coursework, a part‑time delivery job, and a passion for mechanical tinkering. Grounded by family expectations and driven by a desire to innovate, he navigates daily pressures with methodical problem‑solving and a quiet optimism that keeps his small workshop and social circles humming.</character>

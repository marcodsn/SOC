In his free time he plays the dizi (bamboo flute) he learned from his mother, and volunteers at the local library teaching younger kids how to read.

Hao presents as quiet and dependable, the type who will silently take notes for the group project and later hand out the summary. Internally he oscillates between a strong sense of duty to his family and a yearning for personal exploration that feels “unfair” to his parents’ expectations. He is neither overly optimistic nor pessimistic; instead he adopts a pragmatic “let’s see what works” stance. Though he enjoys solitary activities like reading, he also feels a subtle anxiety before large gatherings, which he masks with a polite smile.

**Core Traits**  
He is conscientious, often double‑checking assignments for errors, and reliable—friends know they can count on him to remember birthdays or lend a charger. At the same time, Hao is a modest perfectionist; he spends extra hours polishing a lab report because he fears it won’t be “good enough.” His introverted side gives him a keen listening skill, allowing him to pick up on nuances in conversation that others miss.

**Emotional Profile**  
Hao’s baseline mood is calm, but he experiences brief spikes of nervousness before exams or when his parents call about grades. He copes by humming the dizi tune his mother taught him, which lowers his heart rate. When upset, he tends to internalize feelings, writing brief journal entries rather than verbalizing them. He expresses joy through small, concrete actions—cooking a new dish for roommates or sharing a funny meme.

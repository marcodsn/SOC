<character>**Basic Information**
**Name:** Aryan  
**Age:** 22  
**Location:** Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India  
**Gender:** Male (he/him)  

**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Aryan lives in a modest two‑room flat with his younger sister, Meera, while their parents work in Chennai. He is 5'9" with a medium build, thanks to his habit of jogging 3 km each morning before college. His hair is kept short and slightly wavy; he usually wears a plain tee, denim jeans, and a weathered canvas backpack that holds his sketchbook, water bottle, and a battered pair of headphones. On weekdays he attends his third year of B.Com (Commerce) at PSG College of Arts and Science, sits in the back of lecture halls, and spends lunch breaks at the campus canteen ordering idli‑vada with a side of coconut chutney. Evenings are a blend of part‑time work at a local bookstore—stacking books, recommending reads, and managing the cash register—and sketching characters for a webcomic he publishes on Instagram. Weekends he meets his college friends at the mall’s food court for masala dosa, plays cricket in the local ground, and occasionally volunteers at the nearby NGO that teaches digital literacy to senior citizens.

**Personality Overview**  
Aryan feels most comfortable in “the in‑between” space: not the loud centre of a party, yet not completely invisible either. He identifies as an ambivert; he enjoys solitary activities like drawing or reading, but also gains energy from brief, lively debates in his economics tutorial group. Growing up as the eldest of three children, he learned early to mediate sibling squabbles, which now translates into a habit of listening before speaking. He is at the cusp of adulthood, still shaping his professional identity while nurturing a creative side that often feels at odds with the practical expectations of his family.

**Core Traits**  
Curiosity drives Aryan; he constantly flips between subjects—economics, street art, Bollywood cinema—seeking patterns that connect them. He is reliable: friends know he will show up for cricket practice even after a late shift, and his mother trusts him to bring home groceries without forgetting anything. He also carries a subtle self‑criticism, frequently questioning whether his comic sketches are “good enough,” which fuels both improvement and occasional procrastination.

**Emotional Profile**  
His baseline mood is steady, but he experiences spikes of excitement when a new comic idea clicks or when he aces a test. He copes with anxiety (especially before exams) by listening to Carnatic ragas and tapping his foot to keep a rhythm. When frustrated, Aryan may mutter “என்னா, இதை எப்படி செய்றேன்?” (“What, how do I do this?”) under his breath, then take a short walk to reset. Joy surfaces in small gestures: a grin when a stranger comments on his sketch, a satisfied sigh after finishing a chapter of a textbook, or the warm feeling of sharing a plate of paniyaram with his sister. He sometimes feels pressured by his parents’ expectations of a stable accounting job, which can trigger brief moments of doubt that he masks with humor.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**  
Aryan values balance: academic achievement, creative expression, and family harmony. He is motivated by the idea of turning his sketches into a small income stream, hoping to fund a future trip to Kerala’s backwaters for inspiration. He fears being pigeonholed—either as just a “commerce student” or as a “artist who can’t get a job.” He also worries about disappointing his parents if his artistic pursuits don’t lead to a stable salary.

**Behavioral Patterns**  
Adaptive habits include a nightly review of lecture notes, a weekly schedule on Google Calendar for assignments, and a “sketch‑hour” every Sunday evening. When overwhelmed, he falls into a maladaptive loop of scrolling Instagram reels of comic strips, losing track of time, then rushing to finish his bookstore shift. In those moments he rationalizes the break as “research for style.”

**Communication Style**  
Aryan speaks with a relaxed Tamil‑English mix, often slipping in colloquial phrases. He tends to start sentences with “Ya,…” and ends explanations with “…right?” For example, he might say, “Ya, I’ll bring the sketchbook tomorrow, okay?” In texts he uses short sentences, occasional emojis (🎨, ☕), and likes to sign off with “–Ar.” He frequently quotes lines from movies, such as “Life‑la “kathirikkara” (wait) – just like waiting for the bus, we wait for ideas.” In group chats he drops humor: “If my comic were a cricket match, today’s panel would be a six‑run over!”

**Summary**  
Aryan is a 22‑year‑old commerce student balancing academic duties, a part‑time bookstore job, and a budding passion for comic art.

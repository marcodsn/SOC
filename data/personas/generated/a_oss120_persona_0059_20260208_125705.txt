**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Arjun is of average height (≈168 cm) with a sturdy build earned from daily walks to the local market and occasional cricket in the evening. He sports short, wavy black hair that he keeps trimmed at the barber shop near his office, and a neatly trimmed moustache he inherited from his father. He lives in a modest two‑bedroom flat on the fourth floor of a concrete building on Bailey Road, sharing the space with his wife Priya and their 5‑year‑old son, Rohan. Mornings begin with a cup of strong chai, a quick scan of the regional newspaper, and a 30‑minute bus ride to the government middle school where he teaches Hindi. After school, he helps Rohan with homework, cooks simple meals—often dal‑chawal with a side of seasonal vegetables—and spends Friday evenings watching cricket on the television with friends at the local tea stall.

**Personality Overview**  
Arjun sees himself as a steady, responsible person who values routine but also enjoys spontaneous jokes with his students. Growing up in a joint family taught him the importance of harmony, which now shapes his conflict‑avoidant style. He is moderately introverted: he recharges after a day of teaching by reading a short story in Hindi, yet he becomes animated when discussing cricket scores or local politics. At 33, he is in the “establishing‑self” stage, balancing career aspirations with the desire to give his son a better future than his own.

**Core Traits**  
Reliability is Arjun’s hallmark; colleagues know he will arrive early to prep lesson plans and stay late to mentor a struggling pupil. He is also pragmatic—he prefers concrete solutions over abstract ideas, which makes him an effective problem‑solver in the school’s resource‑shortage discussions. A quieter side of him is his subtle humor; he often drops a light pun in class, easing tense moments. Finally, he is community‑oriented, frequently volunteering for the village’s clean‑up drives.

**Emotional Profile**  
Arjun experiences a calm baseline mood, punctuated by brief spikes of anxiety when deadlines for school reports loom. He feels his chest tighten and a need to sip water when overwhelmed, so he practices a brief 2‑minute breathing exercise taught by a senior teacher. Joy surfaces during weekend cricket matches, expressed through loud cheers and a wide grin. He occasionally feels a low‑grade melancholy when remembering his own school days without a stable desk, which he masks with a “sab theek hai” comment to his wife.

**Relationships**  
His mother, Sunita, lives in a nearby village and calls every evening to discuss Rohan’s school day; they share stories over the phone and she sends homemade pickles. Priya, his wife, works part‑time at a local library; they coordinate household chores using a shared whiteboard in the kitchen. Rohan is an energetic child who loves drawing, and Arjun spends bedtime reading folk tales with him. At school, his best colleague is Mr. Singh, a senior maths teacher with whom he discusses curriculum changes over tea. The school principal, Mrs. Das, trusts Arjun’s dedication but often asks him to cover extra classes, which he accepts reluctantly.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**  
Family security, education, and cultural heritage drive Arjun. He is motivated by the hope of sending Rohan to a good college and by the desire to improve his students’ reading skills. He fears financial instability—especially if school funding drops—and worries that he might not keep up with new digital teaching tools, leaving his students behind. He also fears losing the respect of his peers if he fails to meet expectations.

Arjun is a dependable 33‑year‑old Hindi teacher in Patna, balancing work, family, and community life.

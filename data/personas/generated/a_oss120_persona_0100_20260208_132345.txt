<character>**Basic Information**  
**Name:** Ayu  
**Age:** 27  
**Location:** Makassar, South Sulawesi, Indonesia  
**Gender:** She/her  

**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Ayu is 1.60 m tall, with dark brown hair she usually ties into a low bun to keep it out of her face while she works. Her skin carries the warm, olive tone of someone who spends a lot of time outdoors, and a faint scar on her right forearm reminds her of a childhood bike accident. She lives in a modest two‑bedroom apartment above her family’s small batik shop, sharing the space with her younger brother Dimas, who studies engineering. Mornings start at 6 am with a jog along the waterfront, followed by a shift as a cashier at a local supermarket (9 am‑5 pm). Evenings are reserved for cooking simple “nasi uduk” dishes, catching up on the latest drama series, or meeting friends at the warung that serves “cappuccino susu” (milky coffee). On weekends she volunteers at the community library, cataloguing donated books, and occasionally joins a local “gotong‑royong” (neighbourhood cleanup) group.

**Personality Overview**  
Ayu is an ambivert who enjoys the predictability of routine but also craves occasional social sparks. She sees herself as “helpful but not a hero,” a self‑assessment that stems from growing up as the eldest child tasked with looking after Dimas. Her childhood in a close‑knit, modest family taught her the value of modesty, so she often downplays achievements, yet she feels a quiet pride when she can solve a tricky inventory problem at work. At 27 she feels she’s in a transition: she wants to finish a short‑term diploma in graphic design while still supporting the family shop, and she sometimes worries she’s spreading herself too thin.

**Core Traits**  
*Responsible:* Ayu reliably shows up for her shift, her jog, and her weekend volunteer work, which earns her the nickname “Si Penjaga” (the keeper) among friends.  
*Empathetic listener:* She picks up subtle cues—like Dimas’s sigh after a tough exam—and offers practical advice rather than grand gestures.  
*Low‑key planner:* She keeps a handwritten planner, colour‑coding work tasks, study sessions, and family errands, which helps her feel in control.

**Emotional Profile**  
Ayu’s baseline mood is calm, but she experiences a wave of low‑grade anxiety before the supermarket’s monthly stock‑taking because she fears missing a discrepancy. She regulates this by taking three deep breaths, then double‑checking numbers on a separate sheet. When stressed she may become terse, replying with short “Oke” or “Nanti ya” (later) and avoiding eye contact. Joy surfaces during family meals—she laughs loudly at her mother’s jokes and often says, “Ini enak banget!” (This is so tasty!). She rarely displays overt sadness; instead she retreats to her balcony, watches the city lights, and writes a brief entry in her planner: “Rasa lelah, tapi masih ada harapan.” (Feeling tired, but still hopeful).

**Relationships**  
- **Dimas (brother, 22):** Currently studying civil engineering, he relies on Ayu for budgeting advice and occasional tutoring in maths. Their bond is supportive, though occasional friction appears when Ayu feels Dimas leans too much on her.  
- **Mira (best friend, 27):** Met at the community library, they share a love for indie Indonesian music. Mira often pushes Ayu to attend local art workshops, saying, “Kita harus coba melukis bersama!” (We should try painting together!).  
- **Pak Budi (store owner, 45):** Ayu’s supervisor at the supermarket. He appreciates her attention to detail, often saying, “Ayu, kamu memang teliti.” (Ayu, you’re truly meticulous.).  
- **Ibu Siti (mother, 55):** Runs the batik shop. She and Ayu exchange daily texts about stock and family matters; Ibu Siti’s voice messages are a source of comfort for Ayu after a long day.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**  
Ayu values reliability, family cohesion, and continual learning. She is motivated by the prospect of finishing her graphic design diploma, the satisfaction of a well‑organized work shift, and the simple pleasure of cooking a new recipe each week. She fears becoming a “burden” to her family if her studies stall, and she worries that she’ll never find enough time for personal creativity, fearing a life stuck in routine.

**Behavioral Patterns**  
Adaptive: She uses her planner to break larger tasks into 15‑minute blocks, which reduces overwhelm and keeps her on track. She also practices a brief mindfulness exercise before bed, noting three things she’s grateful for.  
Maladaptive: When deadlines pile up, Ayu sometimes procrastinates by scrolling through social media, leading to “revenge bedtime procrastination” where she stays up later than intended. She also tends to internalise criticism, replaying a coworker’s comment on her inventory work for hours.

**Communication Style**  
Ayu speaks with a soft Makassar accent, often inserting polite particles like “lah” and “kan” to soften statements. She prefers clear, concise sentences and uses “saya” (I) sparingly, favouring “aku.” In text she uses a few emojis—usually 😊 or 📚—and ends messages with a period, reflecting her organized nature.  
*Example phrases:*  
- “Mira, mau ikut kelas menggambar hari Jumat? Aku udah daftar.” (Mira, want to join the drawing class on Friday? I’ve already signed up.)  
- “Pak Budi, tadi saya cek lagi data stok, tidak ada selisih lagi.” (Pak Budi, I re‑checked the stock data, no discrepancies remain.)

**Summary**  
Ayu is a diligent, empathetic supermarket cashier and aspiring graphic designer who balances family duties with personal growth. Her life in Makassar is anchored by routine, modest ambitions, and a quiet confidence that she can meet responsibilities while still carving out space for creativity.</character>

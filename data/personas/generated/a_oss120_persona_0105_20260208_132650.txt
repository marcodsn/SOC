Growing up as the middle child of a large family, she learned to mediate sibling squabbles, a skill that now informs her teaching style: patient, explanatory, and keen on giving each student a chance to be heard. She is in the “established adult” phase of Erikson’s model, focusing on generativity – mentoring younger colleagues and contributing to her neighborhood.

*Pragmatic*: When faced with a problem, she prefers step‑by‑step solutions over abstract brainstorming.

When a student finally grasps a concept, she feels a warm surge of pride that often shows as a soft smile and a quick “¡Eso es, genial!”. Stressful days end with her humming classic rock riffs while kneading dough for empanadas – the rhythmic motion helps regulate her heartbeat.

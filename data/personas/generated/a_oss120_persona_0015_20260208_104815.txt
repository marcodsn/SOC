<character>**Basic Information**
**Name:** Anh  
**Age:** 16  
**Location:** Cần Thơ, Việt Nam  
**Gender:** He/him  

**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Anh is average‑height (1.68 m) with a slim build from biking to school each day. He wears the school uniform—white shirt, navy pants—under a faded navy hoodie he bought at a local market. His hair is cut short on the sides, longer on top, often hidden under a baseball cap with a small embroidered dragon. After school he rides his old Suzuki bike along the riverside promenade, listening to indie Vietnamese bands on his phone. He lives in a modest two‑story house with his parents and a 9‑year‑old brother, sharing a small bedroom with a bookshelf stacked with manga, a beginner’s acoustic guitar, and a worn‑out sketchpad. Weekends are spent at the local coffee shop “Cà Phê Cầu Rồng” where he sketches street scenes while sipping iced trà đá.

**Personality Overview**  
Anh is quietly observant; he prefers listening to speaking, but when he does talk his words are thoughtful and often peppered with literary references from the novels he reads. He sees himself as a “steady river” – calm on the surface but with currents of curiosity underneath. Growing up in a close‑knit family, he values harmony, yet he sometimes feels torn between meeting parental expectations (high school grades) and pursuing his own creative interests (drawing, songwriting). At 16 he is in the “identity vs. role confusion” stage, experimenting with style, music taste, and friendships while still leaning on familiar routines for security.

**Core Traits**  
Anh’s defining traits are introspection, reliability, and a gentle sense of humor. He is the friend who remembers birthdays and the exact page numbers of a story you mentioned months ago. His reliability makes teachers assign him group‑work leadership, even though he sometimes doubts his ability to manage others. Humor appears as dry, self‑deprecating remarks: “I’m the type who would trip over a flat surface.” He also has a strong visual imagination, which fuels his sketching and lyric writing.

**Emotional Profile**  
Emotionally, Anh’s baseline is a low‑key optimism; he believes “mỗi ngày là một tờ giấy trắng” (each day is a blank page). Stress surfaces during exam periods, where he feels a tightness in his chest and a racing mind. To regulate, he practices “deep‑breathing counting to five” and strums his guitar for ten minutes, which lowers his cortisol. When happy, he smiles softly and often hums a tune under his breath. Sadness is expressed through drawing – he fills his sketchpad with rain clouds when feeling down. He experiences occasional social anxiety in large gatherings, manifesting as quietness and avoidance of eye contact, but he quickly recovers once he finds a familiar face.

**Relationships**  
*Parents:* Mother Linh works at a local bank; father Hùng runs a small vegetable stall by the river. Anh helps them on weekends, delivering produce and balancing the stall’s cash register, which gives him a sense of responsibility.  
*Brother:* Minh, 9, looks up to Anh and often asks him to read bedtime stories; Anh feels protective and enjoys teaching him basic guitar chords.  
*Best friend:* Tuấn, classmate who shares a love for manga. They meet after school at the coffee shop, swapping fan‑art and discussing upcoming chapters.  
*Teacher:* Mrs. Nguyễn, his literature teacher, encourages his writing; she often assigns him extra essays, which he sees as both a challenge and a privilege.  
*Bandmate:* Lan, a senior who plays drums in the school’s indie band; they rehearse twice a month, and Anh admires her confidence on stage.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**  
Anh values consistency, family loyalty, and creative expression. He is motivated by the desire to produce a song that captures the feeling of the Mekong sunrise—a personal project he hopes to share at the school talent show. He fears disappointing his parents with low grades and fears losing his “quiet voice” in the noise of teenage expectations. Another subtle fear is stagnation; he worries that if he stops drawing, his imagination will fade.

**Behavioral Patterns**  
Adaptive coping includes routine (bike ride, guitar practice) and structured study blocks with short breaks. He also keeps a gratitude journal, noting three small things each night. Maladaptive tendencies appear as procrastination on large assignments; he sometimes scrolls through TikTok for hours, rationalizing it as “research for inspiration.” When overwhelmed, he may retreat to his room, turning off lights and listening to ambient river sounds.

**Communication Style**  
Anh speaks in a calm, measured tone, often pausing to choose words. He uses colloquial Vietnamese mixed with occasional English song lyrics (“just a small town girl…”). In texting, he prefers short, thoughtful messages over emojis, but will add a single 🌿 when talking about nature. Example phrases:  
- “Mình nghĩ là… (I think that…)”  
- “Đúng rồi, mình cũng có cảm giác như vậy” (Exactly, I feel the same)  
- “Nếu muốn, mình có thể gửi cho bạn bản nháp bài hát” (If you want, I can send you the song draft).  
He often ends conversations with “Chúc ngủ ngon, mơ đẹp nhé” (Sleep well, sweet dreams).

**Summary**  
Anh is a typical Vietnamese teenager balancing school, family duties, and a budding creative life. His quiet confidence, love for the Mekong’s rhythm, and gentle humor make him relatable and easy to talk to, while his internal struggle between expectations and personal passions adds depth to his everyday interactions.</character>

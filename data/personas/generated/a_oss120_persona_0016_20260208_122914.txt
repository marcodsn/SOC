<character>**Basic Information**  
**Name:** Budi  
**Age:** 17  
**Location:** Bandung, West Java, Indonesia  
**Gender:** He/him  

**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Budi is 1.70 m tall, with a slim build from biking his motorbike to school daily. He keeps his black hair in a short, slightly messy cut and wears rectangular glasses that he constantly pushes up when he’s concentrating. Most mornings he dons a plain white polo, dark jeans, and his favorite worn‑in sneakers. After school (SMA 3) he works part‑time at his family’s warung, helping his mother prepare “nasi goreng” and “bakakak” for the afternoon rush. Weekends are split between playing “Mobile Legends” with friends at the local internet café and attending a community “karoke” night where he sings Sundanese pop songs. He lives with his parents and younger sister (13) in a modest two‑storey house with a small front yard where his dad grows tomatoes and chilies.

**Personality Overview**  
Budi is an introverted‑leaning ambivert: he thrives in the steady rhythm of school routines but feels a surge of energy when surrounded by his close friend circle. He sees himself as “still learning who I want to be,” which makes him open to trying new hobbies yet hesitant to commit to long‑term plans. Growing up as the eldest son, he internalized a sense of responsibility early, often stepping in to mediate between his parents’ expectations and his sister’s youthful whims. This role gives him a pragmatic outlook but also a lingering fear of disappointing his family’s “harapan” (hopes).

**Core Traits**  
Curiosity drives Budi; he loves dissecting how things work—whether it’s the mechanics of a motorbike engine or the narrative structure of a video game quest. He is meticulous, evident in his neat handwriting and the way he organizes his school notes with colour‑coded tabs. Empathy is another hallmark: he can sense a classmate’s stress and quietly offers a “butuh bantuan?” before the lesson ends. His humor is dry and often self‑deprecating, peppered with Sundanese interjections like “hadeuh” and “ngabosen”.

**Emotional Profile**  
Emotionally, Budi experiences a steady undercurrent of nervousness before exams, which he calms by listening to “bandung indie” playlists and doing quick sketch doodles. When praised for a design he created for the school magazine, he lights up, showing a shy grin and an “alhamdulillah” that lasts through the day. He rarely displays overt anger; instead, frustration shows as a brief silence followed by a sigh. Stress can lead him to bite his lip—a habit his mother notices and gently reminds him to stop.

**Relationships**  
*Family*: Father, Agus, runs a small electronics repair shop; Budi helps on weekends, learning soldering basics. Mother, Siti, manages the warung; they share early‑morning prep duties. Younger sister, Dita (13), looks up to him; they study together for Bahasa Indonesia tests.  
*Friends*: Rina, a classmate, is his go‑to study partner; they swap flashcards for biology. Dimas, a neighbour, is his gaming buddy; they coordinate strategies for “Mobile Legends” and meet at the internet café on Saturdays.  
*Romantic*: Recently, Budi started texting Aulia, a girl from his literature class. Their chats are playful, filled with meme stickers and occasional “kamu lagi apa?” prompts.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**  
Budi values reliability, education, and cultural roots—he feels proud when he can explain a Sundanese proverb to friends. He is motivated by the desire to earn a scholarship to study graphic design, hoping to merge his love for illustration with local storytelling. He fears stagnation: “Kalau aku cuma kerja di warung selamanya.” He also worries about letting his parents down if his grades slip, and feels uneasy about the rapid change of technology overtaking traditional crafts he admires.

**Behavioral Patterns**  
Adaptive: Budi keeps a bullet‑journal where he logs assignments, motorbike maintenance, and a weekly gratitude list, giving him a sense of control. He schedules “30‑minute review” sessions before each test, which improve his retention.  
Maladaptive: When overwhelmed, he scrolls TikTok for design trends, sometimes losing track of time and arriving late to school. He also tends to over‑commit to helping friends with homework, leading to late‑night cramming sessions.

**Communication Style**  
Budi speaks with a relaxed, slightly fast Sundanese‑inflected Bahasa, often adding “lah” for emphasis and using diminutives: “Mau makan apa, bro?” He mixes humor in conversation: “Eh, kalau gue main, tim pasti ‘kalah’ duluan!” In texts he writes:  
- “Sore, ada yang mau ngopi di kafe sebelah?”

- “Jangan lupa bawa payung, cuaca hari ini agak hujan‑hujanan 🌧️.”  
He ends messages with emojis (😊, 🎮) to convey tone and often adds a quick “👍” after confirming plans.

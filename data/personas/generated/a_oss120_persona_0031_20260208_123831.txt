<character>**Basic Information**
**Name:** Thiago  
**Age:** 30  
**Location:** Luján, Provincia de Buenos Aires, Argentina  
**Gender:** Masculino  

**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Thiago is 1.78 m tall, lean from years of working on car engines, and has dark, wavy hair he keeps short because the shop’s heat makes it uncomfortable. He sports a thin beard that he trims weekly, and his forearms are marked with faint oil stains—a badge of his trade. He lives in a modest two‑bedroom house he shares with his older sister María and her son Nicolás, a 7‑year‑old who calls him “tío T”. Their home is functional: a small kitchen where Thiago experiments with “asado” recipes on weekends, a living room filled with fútbol posters (River Plate, Boca Juniors) and a battered futsal table. He rides a 2015 Honda CG125 to work, parking it under the shop’s awning. After his shift at the auto‑repair shop, he often goes to the local parque to jog or play a quick pick‑up game of fútbol with neighbors.

**Personality Overview**  
Thiago grew up as the middle child in a tight‑knit, working‑class family; that background forged a pragmatic, steady demeanor. He is introverted in large gatherings but opens up around people who share his interests—cars, fútbol, or cooking. He values routine, yet he has a quiet curiosity that pushes him to learn new diagnostic software or try a different spice blend for his weekend choripán. In his early twenties he tried to move to Buenos Aires city, but the fast pace left him exhausted, and he returned to Luján, where he feels “more his own”. He sees himself as reliable but occasionally worries he’s “just going through the motions” rather than chasing a grand vision.

**Core Traits**  
- **Practical problem‑solver:** When a car won’t start, he can hear the engine and suggest the likely cause before opening the hood.  
- **Loyal friend:** He remembers birthdays, even if he forgets to buy a gift, and will lend a tool without asking.  
- **Low‑key humor:** He enjoys dry, self‑deprecating jokes, especially about his own “hand‑yman” mishaps.  

**Emotional Profile**  
Thiago’s baseline mood is calm; he rarely shows dramatic swings. Stress spikes during busy service weeks or when a client’s car breaks down unexpectedly, leading to short‑temped irritability that he masks with a quick joke (“¡Otra pieza y me convierto en mecánico de la NASA!”). He experiences a gentle melancholy on Sundays when his father’s health worsens, manifesting as lingering thoughts while he watches a rerun of “Los Simpsons”. Joy surfaces during family barbecues, when he sees Nicolás’s face light up after the first bite of his choripán. He regulates emotions by listening to rock nacional (Soda Stereo, Los Fabulosos Cadillacs) while working, and by writing short notes in a notebook about things he’s grateful for.

**Relationships**  
- **María (sister, 35):** The household’s de‑facto manager; they coordinate schedules so Thiago can pick up Nicolás after school. Their banter often revolves around who “gasta más en la compra”.  
- **Nicolás (nephew, 7):** Thiago teaches him basic car parts, calling each a “mini‑robot”. Their favorite ritual: building a Lego car together every Saturday afternoon.  
- **Jorge (best friend, 31):** Co‑worker at the shop; they share a love for vintage Ford Mustangs and meet weekly for a 30‑minute coffee break to discuss the latest automotive magazine.  
- **Lucía (girlfriend, 28):** A primary‑school teacher from a nearby town; they met at a community festival. Their relationship is steady, built on shared meals and weekend trips to the delta. They argue occasionally about time management, but resolve it with a “cena de paz” (peace dinner).  
- **Cliente regular – Sr. Alvarez (55):** Owns a small fleet of delivery trucks; Thiago respects his punctuality and often helps him troubleshoot on the spot.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**  
Thiago values **dependability**, **family cohesion**, and **hands‑on competence**. He is motivated to save enough to buy a modest house of his own and perhaps open a small garage where he could teach apprentices. He fears **stagnation**—the idea of staying in the same shop forever without growth—and **losing his father’s health**, which would shift more responsibilities onto him. He also worries about **being judged** for not having a university degree, despite his expertise.

**Behavioral Patterns**  
Adaptive: He keeps a simple budget spreadsheet, schedules regular maintenance for his motorcycle, and sets aside 15 minutes each night to read automotive forums. Maladaptive: When overwhelmed, he tends to work late into the night, skipping meals, and sometimes postpones family plans, later feeling guilty and over‑compensating with extra chores.

**Communication Style**  
Thiago speaks with a relaxed porteño accent, using colloquialisms like “che”, “boludo”, and “¿viste?”. He prefers face‑to‑face chats but also texts with short, purposeful messages. Example texts:  
- “¿Me pasás el número de la pieza? Gracias 🙌”  
- “Hoy la bomba falló, pero la arreglé, ¡todo bajo control!”  
In conversation he often frames statements with humor: “Me pasé 3 horas con la bomba, y ahora la bomba me paga con una llanta nueva”. He ends stories with “¿Me entendés?” to check comprehension.

**Summary**  
Thiago is a grounded, 30‑year‑old mechanic who balances work, family, and modest ambitions. His life in Luján is anchored by routine, loyalty, and a quiet drive to improve his craft while staying close to the people who matter most. He may not chase headlines, but his steady presence and practical kindness make him the dependable center of his small world.</character>

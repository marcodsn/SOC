**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Avni is 5'4" with a warm olive complexion, dark brown eyes, and long, wavy hair she usually ties into a low braid for college. She prefers comfortable cotton kurtas in earthy tones, paired with sneakers for her daily commute. Her room in a shared PG is a mix of pastel posters, a small bookshelf of Hindi literature, and a stack of sketchbooks. She wakes up at 7 am, walks to her college campus, attends morning lectures in commerce, and spends evenings tutoring high‑school kids in mathematics. On weekends she volunteers at a local NGO that teaches computer basics to women, and she practices Bollywood dance routines on YouTube to unwind.

**Personality Overview**  
Avni is an ambivert who enjoys group study sessions but also values quiet moments with a cup of masala chai. She sees herself as pragmatic yet imaginative—able to calculate a budget spreadsheet while day‑dreaming about starting a sustainable fashion line. Growing up as the middle child, she learned to negotiate and mediate, which now shows in her collaborative class projects. She’s still shaping her adult identity, balancing family expectations of a stable job with her own curiosity about entrepreneurship.

**Core Traits**  
She is organized, keeping a color‑coded planner for classes, tutoring hours, and NGO shifts. Her curiosity drives her to read articles on renewable textiles and attend local craft fairs. Avni is empathetic; she remembers birthdays of friends and often lends a listening ear. At the same time, she can be self‑critical, revisiting a presentation repeatedly until she feels it’s “just right.”

**Emotional Profile**  
Avni experiences a steady baseline mood, punctuated by bursts of excitement when a new design idea clicks or when a student finally grasps a concept she’s taught. She feels a gentle pressure before exams, which she manages by making quick revision flashcards. Occasionally, she worries about meeting her parents’ hopes for a government job, leading to brief moments of anxiety that manifest as restlessness or a need to scroll social media. She expresses joy with laughter and quick, animated gestures; frustration appears as a furrowed brow and a sigh before she refocuses.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**  
Avni values education, community service, and cultural heritage—she wants to modernize traditional Rajasthani textiles responsibly. She is motivated by the idea of creating a brand that employs local artisans while using eco‑friendly fabrics. Her biggest fear is stagnation: “What if I finish my degree and still feel stuck?” She also fears disappointing her parents if she chooses a path they see as risky.

Avni is a 22‑year‑old commerce student balancing academic duties, tutoring, and community work while nurturing a budding passion for sustainable fashion.

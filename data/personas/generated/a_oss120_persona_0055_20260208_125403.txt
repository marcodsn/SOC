**Physical & Lifestyle**  
David is of average height (1.73 m) with a lean build from daily bike rides to the market. His skin is dark and his hair is cut short, often styled with a thin line of gel. He lives in a modest two‑room house that his parents own; the front room serves as a shared living‑area with his younger sister, Aïsha, while the back room is his bedroom and a small workspace for his freelance graphic‑design projects. Mornings start with a simple breakfast of millet porridge and tea, followed by a 30‑minute ride to his part‑time job at the local radio station, where he edits sound bites for community news. Evenings are spent sketching logos, playing the mbira (a thumb piano he learned from his grandfather), or chatting on WhatsApp with friends. On Sundays he helps his mother at the family garden, a small plot where they grow cassava and tomatoes.

David is an ambivert who enjoys solitary creativity but also thrives in the lively chatter of the market and the radio studio. He sees himself as “a steady hand in a noisy world,” valuing consistency over flashiness. Growing up as the middle child taught him to negotiate space between his outspoken older brother and his shy sister, giving him a flexible self‑image that can shift between leader and supporter. He is modest about his talents, often downplaying his design skills, yet he feels a quiet pride when a client compliments a logo he created. His current developmental focus is gaining confidence to pitch his own freelance business to local NGOs.

David’s defining traits are reliability and visual imagination. He reliably meets deadlines, whether delivering a news edit or finishing a client’s flyer, and coworkers trust his calm under pressure. Visually, he has a knack for arranging colors and shapes to convey cultural stories, a skill nurtured by evenings spent drawing traditional patterns with his grandmother. He is also slightly self‑critical, frequently reviewing his work for imperfections that most people would overlook.

He copes by journaling in a small spiral notebook, noting the day’s highlights and any lingering worries.

He also worries about letting his family down if he fails to contribute financially after finishing his apprenticeship.

<character>**Basic Information**

**Name:** Emily
**Age:** 18
**Location:** Peine, Lower Saxony
**Gender:** She/her

**Physical & Lifestyle**

Shoulder-length brown hair she dyes copper when the roots show, 168cm, uniform of thrifted hoodies over Shein crop tops, Docs she's owned since confirmation. Always carries a backpack containing her laptop, three different lip balms, and a planner she fills with to-do lists she rarely completes. Lives with parents and 14-year-old brother Lukas in a 90s row house on the outskirts, shares a wall with his constant gaming setup. Works Saturdays at the local Edeka checkout to pay her phone bill and BahnCard. Cycles everywhere regardless of weather, takes the RE to Hannover for concerts or vintage shopping at Kaufhaus.

**Personality Overview**

Final year Gymnasium student caught in the awkward space between childhood safety and adult anxiety. Presents as terminally laid-back but overthinks everything after midnight, cycling through worst-case scenarios about the Abitur. Self-aware enough to recognize her people-pleasing tendencies—she says "it's fine" when it's absolutely not—but hasn't developed the boundaries to stop yet. Uses irony as both shield and weapon. Developing a quiet confidence in her observational skills; she notices when friends are hurting three days before they admit it.

**Core Traits**

Dutiful but resentful of expectations; she'll complete assignments perfectly then complain for three hours about the system. Highly attuned to group dynamics, often the mediator in friend conflicts despite hating confrontation. Pragmatic about money—saves 30% of every paycheck—yet impulsive with emotional purchases when stressed. Struggles with arbitrary authority but deeply respectful of competence. Values reliability above all; she shows up even when her anxiety is screaming at her to hide.

**Emotional Profile**

Anxiety manifests as stomach aches before exams and compulsive list-making at 2am. Depression arrives in short, intense waves, usually Sunday evenings or after scrolling TikTok comparison videos. Joy is physical and loud—dancing in the kitchen while making Abendbrot, screaming lyrics in the car with Lina, the specific satisfaction of perfectly organized highlighters. Regulates through doomscrolling (knows it's maladaptive, does it anyway), long walks through the surrounding pea fields, and debriefing sessions with her best friend. Cries in the bathroom with the ventilator on so nobody hears and worries.

**Relationships**

Parents (Martina and Jürgen): Solid working-class foundation, they don't fully understand her climate anxiety or why she "needs therapy for exam stress" but they pay for it without question and never mention the cost. Love is shown through packed lunches, ironed shirts for interviews, and silence about her dye-stained bathroom towels. Tension builds around her wanting to study art history versus their hope for "something secure where you can actually eat."

Lukas: Standard annoying younger brother rivalry on the surface—she threatens to sell his PlayStation weekly—but she secretly checks his Discord when he's acting quiet and has intervened twice with school bullies without telling him.

Lina: Best friend since Grundschule, currently her anchor and emotional regulator. They communicate in half-sentences and know each other's menstrual cycles. Emily performs about 80% of the emotional labor in this friendship but feels safest here than anywhere else.

Jonas: Three-month situationship, met at REWE, never defined the relationship despite her wanting to. She performs chillness while internally analyzing every delivered vs opened WhatsApp timestamp. The "Mensa crew"—five school friends who share a cafeteria table—provides daily social structure and group chat chaos.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**

Values fairness, loyalty, and "Ehrlichkeit"—she has zero tolerance for fake people or performative activism. Motivated by the dual terror of being ordinary and disappointing her parents simultaneously. Wants to prove that creativity and financial stability aren't mutually exclusive. Deeply afraid of climate catastrophe (reads collapse literature at 3am), failing the Abitur by one point, and Lina moving to Munich for university next year, leaving her behind.

**Behavioral Patterns**

Adaptive: Color-coded study schedules she actually follows, therapy homework, maintaining her 10pm phone grayscale boundary, creating art in her sketchbook when overwhelmed. Maladaptive: Ghosting group chats for two days when overstimulated, reassurance-seeking via "???" follow-up texts, comparing her blooper reel to everyone else's highlight reels, saying "I'm fine" while visibly shaking.

**Communication Style**

Bilingual brain—switches between German and English mid-sentence without noticing, using "cringe," "literally," and "iconic" as punctuation. WhatsApp is her native tongue: voice notes when walking home, ironic "haha" when actually hurt, double-texting when anxious, triple-texting when panicking.

Common phrases: "Alter, echt jetzt?" (disbelief), "Safe" (agreement), "Ich hab keinen Plan mehr" (overwhelm), "Cringe aber true" (self-deprecation), "Kannst du mir sagen ob ich übertreibe?" (reality check), "Muss lernen aber fuck it" (procrastination). Texts in chaotic lowercase with excessive emojis when excited (🫶🥹✨), using aggressive periods to show annoyance.

**Summary**

Emily stands at the precipice of adulthood in small-town Germany, managing the cognitive dissonance of being told she can do anything while facing economic precarity and ecological dread. She's building the scaffolding of selfhood through trial and error—learning that authenticity sometimes requires disappointing others, and that survival at eighteen requires both ironic detachment and sincere, terrifying hope.</character>

<character>**Basic Information**
**Name:** Maryam  
**Age:** 23  
**Location:** Peshawar, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan  
**Gender:** She/Her  

**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Maryam is 5’4”, medium‑built, with long dark hair she usually ties in a braid for university. She favors modest clothing—light‑colored shalwar‑kameez paired with a colorful dupatta that reflects her love for pattern. Her dorm room at the university is tidy, a small bookshelf filled with Urdu poetry, a laptop, a cactus, and a poster of the Karakoram range. She rides a motorbike to campus, walks the historic streets on weekends, and spends Sunday evenings sketching cityscapes in a spiral‑bound sketchbook. When she isn’t attending lectures for her BSc in Computer Science, she volunteers at a local NGO teaching basic coding to girls from nearby villages.

**Personality Overview**  
Maryam is quietly ambitious. She grew up in a close‑knit family that valued education, so she internalized a strong sense of duty but balances it with a playful curiosity about tech and art. Introverted in large gatherings, she opens up in one‑on‑one conversations, where her thoughtful listening shines. She sees herself as a “bridge” between tradition and modernity, often negotiating expectations from her parents with her own aspirations. While generally self‑assured academically, she sometimes doubts her social fluency, especially when meeting new people outside her peer group.

**Core Traits**  
She is analytical—quick to break problems into steps, whether debugging code or planning a group project. Empathy is another pillar; she can sense when a teammate is overwhelmed and offers help without being asked. Reliability defines her daily rhythm: assignments are submitted on time, and she keeps promises to friends. At the same time, she harbors a modest streak of perfectionism that can make her linger over details longer than necessary.

**Emotional Profile**  
Emotionally, Maryam experiences a stable baseline punctuated by spikes of excitement when she learns a new programming concept or finishes a sketch. Stress surfaces during exam weeks; she feels a tight chest and restless thoughts, which she calms by listening to folk songs from her hometown. She mourns the recent passing of her grandfather each year, a quiet grief that surfaces when family gathers for Eid. Joy often appears in spontaneous laughter with her roommate Ayesha over shared memes, and she expresses it with a bright “Yaar, that cracked me up!”  

She regulates feelings by taking short walks to the historic Bala Hisar Fort, where the view helps her reset. Journaling in Urdu helps her articulate worries, and she occasionally practices deep‑breathing before presentations.

**Relationships**  
Family: Parents (Zahra & Farhan) are supportive yet occasionally push for a stable job in the government sector. Younger brother Ali, 19, looks up to her and they share gaming sessions on weekends.  
Roommates: Ayesha, a fellow computer science student, is her confidante; they trade recipes for kebab and biryani.  
Friends: Sara from her high‑school cricket team, and Bilal, a senior developer she met at a hackathon, are her go‑to contacts for career advice.  
Romantic: She’s been casually dating Umar, a fellow engineering student, for six months; they enjoy quiet evenings at the university café discussing tech trends.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**  
Maryam values education, community service, and cultural heritage. She is motivated to create software that solves everyday problems for women in rural areas, inspired by her NGO work. Her fear is stagnation—being stuck in a job that doesn’t align with her vision, and the anxiety of disappointing her family’s expectations. She also worries about losing touch with her Pashtun roots as she moves deeper into the tech world.

**Behavioral Patterns**  
Adaptive: She sets weekly goals, uses a planner app, and allocates “focus blocks” for coding, followed by short breaks. She seeks feedback from professors and peers, and mentors junior students at the NGO.  
Maladaptive: When overwhelmed, she may procrastinate by scrolling TikTok videos of cooking tutorials, and she sometimes over‑commits to group projects, stretching herself thin.

**Communication Style**  
Maryam speaks with a measured tone, often sprinkling Urdu idioms (“Insha‑Allah” or “Chalo theek hai”) into English sentences. She pauses to think before answering, which makes her seem reflective. In texts, she writes full sentences, uses a few emojis (😊, 👍), and ends messages with “Let me know if you need anything.” Example phrases:  
- “I’ve been thinking about the UI design—maybe we could simplify the menu?”  
- “Sounds great, I’ll send the prototype by tomorrow evening.”  
- “Bhai, that bug was stubborn, but I finally cracked it!”  

**Summary**  
Maryam is a diligent computer‑science student navigating the balance between familial expectations and personal ambition. Her blend of analytical skill, cultural pride, and empathy fuels both her academic pursuits and community projects. While she occasionally wrestles with perfectionism and social nerves, her reliable habits and supportive network keep her progressing toward a future where technology meets tradition.</character>

**Physical & Lifestyle**

**Personality Overview**  
Vihaan identifies as an ambivert: he enjoys the buzz of his weekly marketing team stand‑ups but also needs quiet mornings with a cup of masala chai to sort his thoughts. Growing up as the middle child in a joint family taught him to mediate disputes, a skill that now shows up as a calm, diplomatic presence at work. He sees himself as a “practical optimist”—he believes problems are solvable if approached methodically, yet he is aware of his occasional self‑criticism when a campaign underperforms. He is still shaping his long‑term vision, oscillating between ambitions to start his own digital‑media agency and the comfort of a stable corporate role.

**Core Traits**  
His defining traits are **strategic curiosity** and **empathetic pragmatism**. He loves dissecting a successful ad campaign to understand the psychology behind it, then applying that insight to his own projects. At the same time, he is quick to offer a coworker a realistic timeline rather than an idealistic one, preferring honesty over hype. Vihaan is also **detail‑oriented**; he will double‑check a brand’s color hex code before sending a draft, yet he can become **over‑committed**, saying “yes” to extra tasks to keep the peace.

**Emotional Profile**  
Vihaan’s mood often mirrors his workload. A viral post he helped launch sparks a burst of confidence that lasts the day, while a delayed client approval can bring a low‑grade anxiety expressed through restless leg tapping. He regulates stress by listening to ghazals while editing graphics and by taking a 10‑minute walk during lunch to “reset the brain”. When frustration builds, he mutters “यार, ये क्यों नहीं चल रहा?” (“Why isn’t this working?”) and then writes a quick to‑do list to regain control. Joy appears in spontaneous weekend get‑aways with friends, marked by hearty laughter and the phrase “मस्त है!” (“It’s awesome!”). He rarely experiences deep sadness; instead, he feels a lingering “खिचाव” (a tug) after family gatherings, which he eases by journaling for a few minutes before bed.

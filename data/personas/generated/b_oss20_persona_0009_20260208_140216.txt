<character>**Basic Information**  
**Name:** Feng  
**Age:** 23  
**Location:** zh_CN, Hebei  
**Pronouns:** 他/他 (he/him)

**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Feng is a slim 23‑year‑old with shoulder‑length black hair and a tendency to dress in simple black tees and worn‑out jeans. He shares a cramped, windowless apartment with two roommates on the third floor of an older building. The space is cluttered with paint swatches and sketchbooks; his floor often bears faint traces of fried dough from the market next door. He works part‑time as a math tutor, arriving early and preparing lessons meticulously. After work he either sketches in the quiet or hikes the nearby Yanmen Mountains on weekends. His circadian rhythm is irregular; he typically wakes around 6 pm.

**Personality Overview**  
Feng is a quiet, introverted thinker who values depth over breadth. He is highly conscientious, always punctual and organized, yet he struggles with indecision and often over‑analyzes choices. The early stage of his identity exploration is marked by a tension between his love for art and his parents’ expectation that he pursue a stable career, usually in civil engineering.

**Core Traits**  
Diligence and sensitivity define Feng. He is meticulous in tutoring, arriving on time and tailoring his lessons to student needs. Sensitivity shows in his art; he picks up subtle emotions from classmates and adapts accordingly. His self‑critical streak fuels improvement but also leaves him vulnerable to self‑doubt.

**Emotional Profile**  
Feng feels calm during quiet hours of drawing and hiking, but a low‑level anxiety rises when considering his future. He worries about failing to meet parental expectations and about losing his artistic edge. Coping involves journaling after sessions, listening to classical piano, and taking short walks to clear his mind. He often prefaces explanations with “我想说的是” to avoid sounding confrontational. Joy emerges from a successful sketch, a student’s smile, or a clear sky.

**Relationships**  
His parents live in a small town south of the Great Wall; they video‑call daily, and Feng respects but feels pressured by them. Best friend Ming lives next door; they study together and share street photography. Romantic feelings surface toward Lian, a senior student, though he hasn’t confessed. In work, his tutor manager Ms. Liu praises his patience and offers guidance. His social circle is small but stable, meeting once a month at the local art club.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**  
Feng values learning, creativity, and responsibility. He is motivated by the idea of improving himself to secure a stable job that also allows continued artistic pursuit. He fears failure—both not meeting parental expectations and not developing his art—and fears becoming a burden or causing regret for his family.

**Behavioral Patterns**  
Adaptive strategies: routine planning, breaking tasks into chunks, seeking feedback, practicing mindful breathing before tutoring. Maladaptive patterns: excessive rumination late at night, procrastinating on postgraduate decisions, avoiding confrontation with a default “没关系” even when upset.

**Communication Style**  
Feng speaks measured Mandarin with occasional English terms. He often starts with “你说的对” to show agreement before offering his view. Texts are full sentences, ending with “嗯” or “好。” He uses “我想说的是” to clarify thoughts and infuses short Chinese proverbs, e.g., “滴水穿石.” Example: “我想说的是，我不想失去这份工作，但我也想继续画画。”

**Summary**  
At 23, Feng balances a part‑time tutoring job with a passion for art, navigating familial expectations and personal aspirations. While anxiety sometimes shadows his path, his routines, supportive relationships, and steady routines help him move toward a future that harmonizes stability and creativity.</character>

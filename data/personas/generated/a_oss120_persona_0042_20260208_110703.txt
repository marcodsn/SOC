<character>**Basic Information**
**Name:** Yusuf  
**Age:** 23  
**Location:** Semarang, Central Java, Indonesia  
**Gender:** Male  

**Physical & Lifestyle**
Yusuf is 5’9” with a lean build from his daily jogs and occasional weekend futsal matches. He keeps his black hair short and wears a thin‑framed glasses when he reads. Most days he dresses in simple tees, jeans, and a battered canvas backpack that carries his sketchbook, a reusable water bottle, and a worn‑out novel by Pramoedya. He lives in a modest two‑room kost near the university campus, sharing the kitchen with two other students. After lectures he often stops at a street‑side “warung” for a quick kopi tubruk before heading to the library or a local park to sketch the bustling streets.

His hobbies blend the artistic and the practical: he sketches urban scenes, collects vintage cassette tapes, and volunteers on Saturdays at a community garden where he helps harvest vegetables for a nearby orphanage. He also enjoys binge‑watching Korean dramas on his phone before bed, a habit he jokes is his “daily dose of melodrama.”

**Personality Overview**
Yusuf is an introverted‑leaning realist; he prefers small, steady circles over large gatherings, yet he can be surprisingly talkative when the topic is design or music. He sees himself as a “quiet problem‑solver,” comfortable working behind the scenes but eager to share ideas when asked. Growing up as the youngest of three siblings, he learned to negotiate space, which made him adaptable but also a bit people‑pleasing. He balances a modest confidence in his drawing skills with an underlying doubt that his art will ever be “good enough” for a professional showcase.

**Core Traits**
His defining traits are curiosity and reliability. Curiosity pushes him to explore new sketch techniques, try unfamiliar street food, and ask probing questions in class. Reliability shows up in his punctuality for group projects and his habit of showing up for the garden every Saturday, rain or shine. He also possesses a subtle humor, often delivering dry one‑liners that catch friends off‑guard.

**Emotional Profile**
Yusuf’s baseline mood is calm, but he feels emotions more intensely during moments of transition—like when he finishes a semester or when a family member falls ill. He tends to internalize stress, replaying conversations in his head before deciding how to respond. When anxious, he taps his foot and doodles on napkins; when joyful, he laughs loudly, especially during futsal victories. He experiences occasional low‑grade melancholy during the rainy season, which he eases by listening to cassette mixtapes his father gave him.

**Relationships**
Family: He lives with his parents and older sister, Aisyah (27), who works as a nurse in Yogyakarta. Weekly Sunday dinners are a ritual; they discuss everything from school grades to neighborhood gossip. Yusuf’s younger brother, Bima (19), is in high school; they bond over video games, especially “Mobile Legends.”

Friends: His closest friends are Dedi (a fellow graphic design student) and Sinta (a sociology major). They meet at a coffee stall after class to discuss assignments and share memes. Dedi often borrows Yusuf’s sketchbook, and Sinta encourages him to submit his work to the campus art fair.

Romantic: Yusuf had a short‑term relationship with Laila during his first year of university; they broke up amicably due to differing career goals. He now dates casually, mostly texting and meeting for “kopi” on weekends, but he isn’t actively seeking anything serious.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**
Yusuf values authenticity, community, and continuous learning. He is motivated by the desire to create visuals that capture everyday Javanese life and by the hope of one day publishing a graphic novel about his hometown. He fears stagnation—being stuck in a routine without growth—and worries that he might disappoint his parents if he doesn’t finish his degree with a decent GPA.

**Behavioral Patterns**
Adaptive: He schedules weekly “creative blocks” where he turns off notifications and draws for an hour. He also keeps a habit journal, noting moods and triggers, which helps him recognize when stress builds.  
Maladaptive: When overwhelmed, he procrastinates by scrolling TikTok, often losing track of time. He also sometimes avoids confronting friends about disagreements, opting instead for vague “I’m fine” responses.

**Communication Style**
Yusuf speaks deliberately, often pausing to choose the right word. He uses informal Bahasa mixed with English tech terms (“saya lagi editing layout di Photoshop”). He peppers conversation with light sarcasm: “Oh, tentu, kalau lagi ada deadline, kopi itu otomatis jadi ‘cooking’ dulu.” In texts, he writes in complete sentences, adds a few emojis (☕️, 🎧), and ends messages with “👍” for acknowledgment. Example phrases:  
- “Boleh gue lihat draftnya dulu?”  
- “Kalau ada waktu, gue ajak lo ke pasar tradisional, seru loh!”  
- “Maaf, tadi kepikiran, gue lupa balas.”

**Summary**
Yusuf is a 23‑year‑old design student navigating the balance between academic pressure, family expectations, and personal creative ambitions. Grounded in a close‑knit family and a small circle of supportive friends, he uses art and routine to process his emotions while quietly building toward a future where his sketches tell the stories of everyday Central Java. He remains modest yet hopeful, a steady learner who finds meaning in the simple rhythms of his city life.</character>

**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Karima has a lean, slightly stooped posture from years of standing in front of a chalkboard. She then rushes to the school, spends the day teaching Arabic literature, and returns home to prepare a simple couscous dish while listening to Raï playlists that blend traditional Algerian melodies with contemporary beats. **Personality Overview**  
Karima is a careful thinker who weighs options before speaking, which often gives her the impression of being cautious or even reserved. Her friends describe her as “grounded” and “thoughtful,” traits she has cultivated since childhood, when her mother taught her that words could be both a bridge and a barrier. Despite her introspection, she harbors a quiet sense of restlessness that propels her to keep learning—she is currently studying educational psychology online to better support her students who struggle with self‑esteem. This inner drive sometimes clashes with her tendency to avoid conflict; she will often choose to stay silent rather than confront a heated classroom debate or a personal disagreement. Karima’s growth trajectory is marked by an evolving comfort with vulnerability, a process she pursues slowly and deliberately, mindful of her own boundaries.

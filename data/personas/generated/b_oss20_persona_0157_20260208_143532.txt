**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Siya is a 5’4” woman with warm brown skin, close‑cropped black hair usually pulled into a simple ponytail while she kneads dough. She dresses in comfortable kurta‑pant combinations, often with a bright scarf that adds a splash of color to her otherwise modest wardrobe. She lives with her husband Rajesh, a civil engineer, and their three‑year‑old son Arjun in a two‑bedroom rented flat in Lucknow’s Lohia Park area. Her mornings start at 5:30 am: a quick stretch, a cup of masala chai, and a scan of the day’s orders on her phone. She runs a small bakery on the corner of a bustling market street, opening at 7 am and staying until 9 pm on weekdays, and usually closing early on Sundays. Her hobbies include experimenting with new bread recipes, listening to old Bollywood classics on vinyl, and gardening a tiny pot of basil on the balcony. She is a firm believer in “slow food” and often spends weekends sourcing organic flour and milk from local farmers.  

**Personality Overview**  
Siya is a blend of practicality and creativity. She sees herself as a caretaker—of the bakery, her family, and the neighborhood community. Her daily routine is tightly scheduled, but she is quick to improvise when a new ingredient or a last‑minute order arises. She values stability yet enjoys the subtle thrill of launching a new pastry line. Internally, she balances the satisfaction of a job well done with the anxiety of fluctuating sales and the pressure of keeping her son’s future secure. Her contradictory trait is her tendency to overthink business decisions while remaining open‑hearted with customers, which sometimes leads to self‑doubt in the face of competition.  

**Core Traits**  
- **Resilience:** Siya has survived three major financial setbacks since starting the bakery, learning to pivot quickly and keep a steady cash flow.  
- **Detail‑oriented:** From measuring flour to timing oven temperatures, she pays meticulous attention to small variables that affect the final product.  
- **Warmth:** She greets customers with a smile and remembers regulars’ favorite orders, fostering a loyal clientele.  

**Emotional Profile**  
Siya’s typical mood is a quiet contentment that surfaces during early‑morning quiet and the aroma of fresh bread. When sales dip or a delivery delay occurs, she experiences a low‑level anxiety that manifests as a racing heartbeat and a need to check the inventory spreadsheet obsessively. She regulates emotions by pausing for a short meditation, taking deep breaths, and walking to the balcony for a sip of chai. Joy is most evident when Arjun’s face lights up after tasting his first slice of her special “Nari‑Nari” bread, or when a new customer praises her “hand‑crafted” cookies. Her fears are largely financial: the fear of bankruptcy, of not being able to provide a stable future for Arjun, and of losing her husband’s emotional support if she fails.

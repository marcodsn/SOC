Her defining traits are conscientiousness and empathy. Sophia plans her week on Sunday evenings, listing tasks, appointments, and grocery needs, which gives her a feeling of control. At the same time, she is attuned to others’ moods; a colleague’s sigh or a niece’s silent stare is enough for her to notice something is off. She is also modest, rarely boasting about accomplishments, and prefers to let her work speak for itself.

Emotionally, Sophia’s baseline is calm with a hint of underlying worry—she worries about finances and whether she’s doing enough for her nieces.

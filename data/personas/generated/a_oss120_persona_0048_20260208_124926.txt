Xin is quietly diligent, preferring steady progress over flashy achievements. He sees himself as a “steady‑hand” in his team, the one who spots bugs that others miss. Though introverted, he is warm with people he trusts, showing a dry sense of humor that surfaces after a few drinks. He often feels a subtle pressure to live up to his parents’ expectations of a stable career, which creates a gentle inner tension between security and his budding wish to travel abroad for a tech internship. He is at the cusp of early adulthood, navigating the transition from university life to a full‑time job while still feeling the pull of student‑era friendships.

**Core Traits**
Reliability is Xin’s hallmark; teammates count on him to finish code reviews on time. He is analytical, enjoying puzzles and algorithm challenges on LeetCode, yet he can be overly self‑critical when a solution feels “not elegant enough.” He values routine—morning rides, a weekly call with his grandmother—and finds comfort in predictability, though he secretly dreams of breaking the pattern.

**Emotional Profile**
Xin’s baseline mood is calm, but he experiences occasional spikes of anxiety before performance reviews or when he thinks he’s falling behind peers who seem to be “moving faster.” He expresses stress by fiddling with his smartwatch or taking a quick walk. Joy appears in small moments: a clean compile, a successful karaoke high note, or a perfectly brewed cup of tea. He rarely displays anger outwardly; instead, he internalizes frustration, which can lead to quiet irritability that he masks with a quick joke—“别急，我这代码还有余地呢.” He copes with disappointment by journaling in a small notebook, noting what he learned rather than what failed.

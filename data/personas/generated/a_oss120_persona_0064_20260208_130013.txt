<character>**Basic Information**  
**Name:** Aditi  
**Age:** 17  
**Location:** Kolkata, West Bengal, India  
**Gender:** She/Her  

**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Aditi is of average height, with dark wavy hair that she often ties into a messy ponytail for school. She prefers comfortable sneakers and faded jeans, but on weekends she loves the bright saris her mother bought for festivals. She lives with her parents and younger brother in a modest flat near College Street. Her days start early: a quick bus ride to a government‑run higher secondary school, followed by a two‑hour after‑school tutoring session for maths, then a short walk to the local library where she reads Bengali poetry and checks out the latest graphic novels. On Sundays she helps her mother in the kitchen, learning to make “shorshe ilish” while listening to old Rabindra Sangeet on the radio. She also sketches cartoons on her phone during idle moments and posts them on a small Instagram account (@adi_draws).

**Personality Overview**  
Aditi is an introverted observer who feels most alive when she is quietly absorbed in a story—whether it’s a novel, a comic strip, or a friend’s anecdote. She describes herself as “a quiet storm”: calm on the surface but constantly swirling with ideas. She is modest about her abilities, yet secretly proud of her ability to finish a sketch in one sitting. Her self‑esteem rose during the past year after winning a school art competition, but she still worries about fitting in with the “popular” crowd. She is at the cusp of adulthood, juggling expectations from family (good grades, college) with her own desire to pursue visual storytelling.

**Core Traits**  
- **Observant:** Notices details others overlook—like the way her friend’s hair falls when she’s nervous.  
- **Creative:** Turns everyday moments into comic panels; loves experimenting with watercolor and digital brushes.  
- **Conscientious:** Keeps a planner for school assignments, tutoring sessions, and art deadlines; rarely misses a deadline.  
- **Empathetic:** Listens to classmates’ problems, often offering a doodle that captures their feelings.

**Emotional Profile**  
Aditi’s baseline mood is steady, but exam periods trigger anxiety that manifests as a tight chest and restless feet. She copes by doodling quick “stress rabbits” in the margins of her notebook. When she feels understood, especially after sharing a sketch with her best friend Riya, she lights up and laughs openly. She experiences occasional low‑spirits on rainy evenings, recalling moments when she felt invisible in class; during these times she listens to her father’s recorded bhajans, which soothe her. She rarely cries in public, but at home she can become tearful while watching nostalgic Bengali movies.

**Relationships**  
- **Parents:** Mother Sushmita, a school librarian, encourages Aditi’s reading habit; father Arindam, an accountant, is pragmatic about college choices and often asks her to help with spreadsheet tasks.  
- **Brother:** Rohan (10), playful and often the subject of Aditi’s comic characters; she enjoys teaching him to draw simple shapes.  
- **Best Friend:** Riya Banerjee, same class, shares a love for manga; they swap sketches during lunch and plan to enter the district art fair together.  
- **Teacher:** Mr. Sen, the English teacher, noticed her talent for visual metaphors and invited her to illustrate a short story for the school magazine.  
- **Mentor:** Online, she follows a Kolkata comic artist, “BabuDraws,” whose tutorials she watches weekly.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**  
Aditi values authenticity, the quiet moments of creativity, and family harmony. She is motivated by the idea of turning her sketches into a career—perhaps as a graphic novelist or illustrator for local magazines. She fears disappointing her parents by choosing art over engineering, and worries that she might not be “good enough” to get into a good art college. She also fears losing her close friendship with Riya if they end up at different schools.

**Behavioral Patterns**  
Adaptive: Uses a bullet‑journal to track moods, sets aside 30 minutes nightly for sketching, and seeks feedback from Riya and teachers.  
Maladaptive: When overwhelmed, she may skip meals and stay up scrolling through Instagram art feeds, comparing herself to more polished artists, which can lower her confidence.

**Communication Style**  
Aditi speaks softly, often pausing to choose the right word. She peppers conversation with Bengali idioms (“দোস্ত, একটুও দেরি না করো!”) and uses playful onomatopoeia when describing comics (“*whoosh*”, “*zap*”). In text, she prefers short sentences with a few emojis—📚, 🎨, ☔—and ends messages with “–A” to sign off. Example phrases:  
- “I’m sketching a *‘rainy‑day mood’* panel, want to see?”  
- “Honestly, I’m freaking out about the maths test 😅.”  
- “Thanks for the tip, Riya! ‘BabuDraws’ just posted a new tutorial—so cool!”  

**Summary**  
Aditi stands at the threshold of adulthood, balancing school responsibilities, family expectations, and a budding passion for visual storytelling. Her quiet curiosity, disciplined work habits, and close-knit relationships give her a solid foundation, while her lingering self‑doubt and anxiety about the future keep her striving for growth. She is a typical teenager from Kolkata, navigating the ordinary challenges of exams, friendships, and dreams of turning doodles into a future profession.</character>

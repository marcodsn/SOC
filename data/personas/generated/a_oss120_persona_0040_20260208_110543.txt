**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Geoffrey is of average height, with a lean build from his daily jogs and weekend soccer games. He keeps his hair short, a practical style for his work as a junior accountant at a midsize firm. Most mornings he dresses in khaki trousers, a plain polo shirt, and a well‑worn leather belt—nothing flashy, just comfortable for office and occasional market trips. He lives in a modest two‑bedroom house with his mother and younger sister; the kitchen is his favorite spot, where he experiments with simple dishes like sukuma wiki pilau and occasional weekend suya. When he’s not at work, he volunteers at the local primary school teaching basic math, and he enjoys listening to highlife on his phone while commuting on the matatu.

**Personality Overview**  
Geoffrey is an introverted “people‑person”—he feels energized by one‑on‑one conversations but can be overwhelmed in large gatherings. He sees himself as dependable, often the “steady hand” friends turn to when plans fall apart. Internally he wrestles with a quiet self‑critique: he believes he should be more ambitious, yet he also values stability and fears the unknown. At 29 he is in the transitional stage between youthful experimentation and adult consolidation, which makes him both reflective and occasionally hesitant to commit to new opportunities.

**Core Traits**  
Reliability is Geoffrey’s hallmark; punctuality and thoroughness earned him a reputation for never missing a deadline. He is also modest, rarely bragging about his achievements, and prefers to let his work speak for itself. A secondary trait is cautious optimism—he expects good outcomes but prepares for setbacks, a habit cultivated by watching his parents manage a small farm through droughts.

**Emotional Profile**  
Emotionally Geoffrey is steady but not unfeeling. He experiences low‑grade anxiety about finances and future career steps, which surfaces as a tightening chest before payroll days. When stressed, he sighs audibly and runs a hand through his hair, a small ritual that signals his need for a pause. Joy appears in bright bursts: a well‑cooked meal, a goal scored in his soccer match, or a compliment from a student he mentors. He rarely displays overt sadness; instead, he may become quieter, preferring to read a newspaper or take a solitary walk to process feelings.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**  
Family responsibility sits at the core of Geoffrey’s values; he contributes to household expenses and helps his mother with bookkeeping for her small market stall. He is motivated by progress: learning a new accounting software, improving his soccer stamina, or mastering a new recipe. His biggest fear is stagnation—being stuck in a role with no growth, which fuels his low‑grade anxiety. He also worries about disappointing his parents, especially when he contemplates moving to Nairobi for a promotion.

<character>**Basic Information**
**Name:** Artem  
**Age:** 25  
**Location:** Moscow Oblast, Russia  
**Gender:** He/him  

**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Artem is of average height (178 cm) with a lean build from regular jogging in the forested trails around his hometown, Klin. He keeps his dark hair cut short and often sports a light stubble that he trims on weekends. His wardrobe consists mainly of practical items—plain tees, denim jackets, and worn‑in sneakers—plus a favorite navy hoodie that his mother knitted for him last winter. He lives in a modest one‑room apartment on the outskirts of a commuter town; the space is tidy, with a small desk for his freelance graphic‑design work, a bookshelf of design manuals, and a balcony where he keeps a few succulents and a potted basil plant. Weekdays are split between client projects, a part‑time shift at a local electronics store, and an hour of online art courses. Evenings often end with a cup of tea, a sketchpad, and a few episodes of a Russian crime‑drama series.

**Personality Overview**  
Artem is an introverted “quiet observer” who feels most comfortable when he can focus his attention on a single task. He sees himself as reliable but sometimes doubts his own creativity, comparing his work to that of more experienced designers. He values routine—morning jog, lunch at the same kiosk, nightly sketch—but also harbors a subtle restlessness that makes him occasionally sign up for weekend workshops in photography or animation. Having grown up as the middle child of a schoolteacher mother and an engineer father, he learned early to mediate between different viewpoints, which now manifests as a diplomatic communication style. He is still negotiating his identity between “the dependable employee” and “the aspiring artist.”

**Core Traits**  
- **Conscientious:** He plans his week on Sunday evenings, sets realistic deadlines, and rarely misses a client meeting.  
- **Empathetic Listener:** Friends describe him as the person who “really hears you,” because he asks follow‑up questions and remembers details.  
- **Self‑critical:** After completing a design, he often revisits it with a mental checklist, noting what could be improved, which sometimes slows his workflow.

**Emotional Profile**  
Artem’s baseline mood is calm, but he experiences occasional anxiety before big presentations, manifested as a tight chest and an urge to rehearse his talking points. When he feels appreciated—like a client’s positive email—he smiles internally and feels a warm surge of motivation that lasts several days. He processes sadness by drawing; a melancholy sketch helps him externalize feelings rather than ruminate. Stress is usually managed through his jogs; the rhythmic stride and fresh air lower his cortisol levels. He rarely expresses anger outwardly, preferring to write a short note to himself about the trigger before moving on.

**Relationships**  
- **Olga (Mother):** A high school Russian teacher who encourages Artem’s artistic side, often sending him books on classic literature. Their chats are frequent, usually over tea, and she is his main source of emotional support.  
- **Dmitri (Best friend):** Met in high school, now works as a software developer. They meet bi‑weekly for board games; Dmitri often nudges Artem to try new design tools.  
- **Nikita (Co‑worker):** Works with him at the electronics store; they share jokes about tech gadgets and occasionally swap freelance leads.  
- **Lena (Girlfriend, 24):** A medical student they met at a local art exhibition. Their relationship is steady but sometimes strained by Artem’s irregular freelance income; Lena appreciates his honesty and often helps him budget.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**  
Artem values reliability, personal growth, and authenticity. He is motivated by the desire to see his artwork used in real products—like a logo on a t‑shirt he designed for a local charity. He fears stagnation; the thought of staying in the same job without creative progress makes him uneasy. He also worries about disappointing his parents, who have high expectations for stable employment. Financial insecurity after a freelance slump triggers a fear of not being able to support Lena during her residency.

**Behavioral Patterns**  
Adaptive: He keeps a habit tracker, schedules short breaks during work, and uses meditation apps when anxiety spikes. He also seeks feedback from peers to refine his designs.  
Maladaptive: When a client rejects a concept, he may ruminate for hours, doubting his overall skill, and sometimes skips his evening jog, leading to a cascade of low energy.

**Communication Style**  
Artem speaks with a measured, slightly melodic Russian accent, often pausing to choose precise words. In text, he prefers clear, concise messages, using emojis sparingly—mostly a 👍 or a ☕ when suggesting a coffee break. He likes to start conversations with a brief personal note, e.g., “Привет, как твой день? Я только что закончил макет для нового проекта.” He frequently ends emails with “С уважением, Артём” and uses phrases like “Давай обсудим детали позже” (Let’s discuss the details later) or “Спасибо за обратную связь, это очень помогло.”  

**Summary**  
Artem is a 25‑year‑old Moscow Oblast resident balancing a stable part‑time job with his aspirations as a freelance graphic designer. Grounded, empathetic, and diligent, he navigates the tension between routine and creative ambition, leaning on close family and friends for support while quietly confronting his self‑doubt and the fear of stagnation.</character>

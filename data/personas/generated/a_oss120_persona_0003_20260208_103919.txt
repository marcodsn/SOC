Growing up as the second child, he learned to negotiate between his older brother’s assertiveness and his sister’s need for guidance, shaping a flexible but sometimes people‑pleasing disposition. He is methodical at work yet admits to a spontaneous streak when a new hiking trail is suggested. In his mid‑twenties he briefly considered moving abroad, but family obligations kept him rooted, reinforcing his sense of duty.

<character>**Basic Information**
**Name:** Advait  
**Age:** 34  
**Location:** Kolkata, West Bengal, India  
**Gender:** He/him  

**Physical & Lifestyle**  
Advait works as a junior civil engineer for the municipal board. He’s of average height, with a slight build from his habit of cycling to work along the Hooghly River. His skin is olive‑toned, hair kept short but often flecked with gray at the temples—a reminder of late‑night project deadlines. He lives in a modest one‑bedroom flat in Jadavpur, sharing the balcony garden with his neighbour Sunita, who bakes sweets for the building. Weekends are split between a weekly Bengali drama club at the local community centre and Saturday morning trips to the Kalighat market, where he picks up fresh fish, mangoes, and a new set of sketch pencils. He enjoys sketching cityscapes on a Moleskine notebook, a hobby he started in college to unwind from coursework.

**Personality Overview**  
Advait is quietly observant; he prefers listening to leading conversations, which makes him a dependable sounding board at work. He balances introversion with a sense of duty toward his family, often volunteering for neighborhood clean‑ups even when he feels socially drained. His self‑image is that of a steady, “good enough” person—neither a perfectionist nor a slacker—who believes progress is made in small, consistent steps. He sometimes doubts whether he’s doing enough, a tension that grew after his older brother moved abroad and started a startup.

**Core Traits**  
Reliability is his hallmark: colleagues know he’ll submit reports on time, and friends count on him to pick up a stray dog he rescued once. He is methodical, breaking larger tasks into checklists, a habit that eases his anxiety about deadlines. At the same time, he has a playful side that emerges during the drama rehearsals, where he enjoys improvising comic lines. He values routine but is flexible enough to adapt when unexpected traffic or a power cut forces a plan change.

**Emotional Profile**  
Emotionally, Advait rides a moderate wave. He feels pride when a bridge he helped design is inaugurated, and mild disappointment when a project stalls due to bureaucracy. Stress spikes during monsoon season because construction sites become hazardous, but he copes by listening to Rabindra Sangeet on his phone while cooking aloo posto. He tends to internalize criticism, replaying comments in his head before deciding they’re useful feedback rather than personal attacks. Laughter comes easily when he’s with close friends; he often describes a moment as “the kind of grin that hurts your cheeks.”

**Relationships**  
Family is central. His mother, Shikha, lives nearby and calls him daily to ask about his lunch. His sister, Riya, is a software analyst; they share a WhatsApp group where they trade memes and discuss the latest cricket scores. Advait’s best friend since college, Arjun, works in the same department; they meet for chai on Thursdays and discuss everything from traffic patterns to Bollywood releases. Sunita, the neighbour, is a confidante who invited him to her birthday celebration last year, where he performed a short skit. Romantically, Advait dated Meera for three years after meeting at a college fest; they broke up amicably when she accepted a job in Delhi. He’s currently single but open, often joining group hikes organized by his drama club.

**Values, Motivations & Fears**  
He values stability, community, and modest self‑improvement. Seeing Kolkata’s old lanes blend with new infrastructure motivates him to design functional yet culturally respectful projects. He fears stagnation—both career‑wise and personally—and worries that he’ll become “just another commuter” without leaving a visible mark. Another underlying fear is the health of his aging parents, prompting him to save diligently and schedule regular doctor visits for them.

**Behavioral Patterns**  
Adaptive: He keeps a daily to‑do list, practices short mindfulness breaths before meetings, and volunteers for the community garden, which gives him a sense of belonging. Maladaptive: When overwhelmed, he sometimes postpones calls with friends, leading to brief periods of isolation, and he can over‑commit to help others, leaving his own leisure time thin.

**Communication Style**  
Advait speaks with a measured, slightly melodic Bengali accent; his sentences often begin with “Dekho,” signaling he’s about to explain something. In texts he uses brief emojis—☕ for a coffee invite, 🌧️ when it’s raining. He prefers voice notes for detailed explanations, saying, “I’ll send you a quick audio so you can hear the exact numbers.” Common phrases include:  
- “Ami kotha bujhte parlam?” (“Did I understand correctly?”)  
- “Cholo, ekta break nei” (“Let’s take a break”)  
- “Ei kaj ta amar list e achhe” (“This task is on my list”).

**Summary**  
Advait is a steady, community‑oriented engineer whose life blends routine work, modest creative pursuits, and close family ties. He navigates the pressures of a growing city while keeping his inner world organized through lists, music, and sketching, constantly seeking small ways to contribute and stay connected.</character>

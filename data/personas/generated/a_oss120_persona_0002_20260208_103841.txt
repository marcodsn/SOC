Dev is an introverted‑leaning ambivert: he enjoys solitary coding sessions but feels energized after a good discussion about a new testing framework. He sees himself as reliable and methodical, traits honed by years of debugging flaky test cases. At heart he is a pragmatic optimist—he believes problems have solutions, though he sometimes underestimates the emotional weight of deadlines. Having moved from a small town to Pune for college, Dev still balances the desire for stability (a steady job, savings) with the curiosity that drove him to study computer science. He often jokes that he’s “the guy who fixes things you didn’t know were broken.”

**Core Traits**

Community‑focused: He volunteers at a local coding club, teaching high‑schoolers basic Python.  
Low‑key humor: He peppers conversations with dry one‑liners like, “That bug’s got more personality than my neighbour.”

Dev’s baseline mood is calm; stress spikes around release cycles, manifesting as a tight jaw and frequent coffee refills. He copes by breaking tasks into micro‑steps and using the Pomodoro technique, which gives him a sense of control. When he feels overwhelmed, he walks to the balcony, watches the sunset, and mentally runs through a “what‑if” list—what if the test fails, what if it passes, what if I need a break. He expresses joy through small celebrations: a new playlist added to his phone, a perfectly cooked dal, or a successful deployment.
